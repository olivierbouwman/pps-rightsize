Christy Splitt
Christy Splitt is a member of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education in Oregon, representing Zone 1. She assumed office in 2025. Her current term ends on June 30, 2027.
Splitt ran in a special election to the Portland Public Schools Board of Education to represent Zone 1 in Oregon. She won in the special general election on May 20, 2025.
