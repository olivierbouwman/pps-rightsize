As a former educator and longtime advocate,
Christy will be the champion that we need for
Portland’s public schools.
- Former State Senator Michael Dembrow
I am the mom of two elementary school students, a former
teacher, a leader in our school’s PTA, and an organizer in
the fight for increased school funding. Public schools are
the foundation of our community, where kids and adults
alike learn from each other and build community. I see the
magic that happens every day, and I also see the barriers
our students and educators face. My priority is to reduce
those barriers and help make every school a joyful, loving
place to grow, learn, and work.
As your school board director, I will:
• Listen to students, families, teachers, administrators, and
support staff
• Promote policies and budgets that center students and
schools
• Advocate for what our students, teachers, and staff need
to succeed
• Champion school funding, racial equity, and social justice
A great city needs great schools. Let’s make it happen!
