Eddie Wang
Eddie Wang is a member of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education in Oregon, representing Zone 7. He assumed office on July 1, 2023. His current term ends on June 30, 2027.
Wang ran for election to the Portland Public Schools Board of Education to represent Zone 7 in Oregon. He won in the general election on May 16, 2023.
