Candidate Name: Eddie Wang
Occupation: Business Owner
Occupational Background: Classroom Teacher, 2001 to 2019; University Supervisor -
Portland State University College of Education, 2018 to 2021
Educational Background: Carleton College B.A., Lewis and Clark College of
Education, Masters in Education.
Prior Government Experience: None
"Eddie was a teacher for 20 years, including at Benson Polytechnic High School in
PPS. He knows what it's like in the trenches and is ready to center teacher voices.
Eddie has a deep understanding of the importance of academic freedom, authentic
community engagement, and holding district leadership accountable. We look forward
to working with him to build great public schools for all students." Portland Association
of Teachers PAC
Eddie's is THE VOICE WE NEED TO:
• Improve Education for All Students- Especially Among Students That Have
Been Historically Disadvantaged.
• Work Towards Racial Equity- We Need to Use Evidence Based Methods and
Strategies, and Not Try to Reinvent the Wheel.
• Improve Our Schools' Working Conditions- Millions of Dollars Are Wasted
Each Year As a Result of Lost Institutional Knowledge. We need to Change the
Culture.
