Voters Pamphlet Statement
Portland City Council, District 2
Michelle DePass
Occupation: Climate Policy Analyst, Portland Bureau of Planning and Sustainability. Occupational Background: Community Engagement, Housing Policy, Energy Efficiency Program Evaluation, Green Building Codes and Standards, Program Management, Program Development Educational Background: Portland Public Schools; BS, Community Development, PSU; Sustainable Business, Bainbridge Graduate Institute Prior Governmental Service: Current Vice Chair and Former Chair Portland Public Schools Board of Education; City of Portland bureaus of Housing, Parks, and Planning and Sustainability; Portland Budget Advisory Committees (Housing and Planning and Sustainability)
A Portland for All
I'm running to represent District 2 because I believe in action with compassion. Whether you are a small business owner, a worker, or a neighbor, I am committed to working to ensure District 2 Portlanders have the tools and support they need to thrive.
Portland needs change
I know Portland can do better. We can make our neighborhoods safe and vibrant; neighbors connected to neighbors, with affordable housing along the spectrum of need, from the visibly houseless to the neighbors at risk of becoming homeless to first-time homebuyers. We can have flourishing business districts and public transportation systems prioritizing safety and climate. Safe neighborhoods are neighborhoods where people are connected and resourced to meet their needs. Our city’s natural beauty is deeply valued, and we must ensure our parks and greenspaces are safe and clean, and the move toward a green economy and climate resilience includes every Portlander.
As a lifelong Portlander with extensive experience in public budgeting, policy development, and climate advocacy, I’m dedicated to collaborating with you and my Council colleagues to create a more inclusive, sustainable, Portland that works for all.
With your vote, I will:
- Expand affordable housing and address houselessness with compassionate, effective solutions
- Ensure safe and efficient transportation systems for all
- Lead the charge on climate justice, ensuring a green future for our city
“An incumbent on the Portland School Board, Michelle is a twice-elected champion of parents, kids, and teachers. VOTE MICHELLE DEPASS for City Council and she will continue to champion this district for you!” - Governor Kate Brown
