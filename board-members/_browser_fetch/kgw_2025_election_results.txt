Clear front-runners emerge in early results for PPS school board races
Four of seven Portland school board seats are up for grabs this election, only two of them with incumbent candidates.

PORTLAND, Ore. — Results started coming in at 8 p.m. Tuesday evening for school board races which could significantly reshape the Portland Public Schools board of directors for the next four years. In early results, Christy Splitt, Rashelle Chase-Miller, Virginia La Forte and Stephanie Engelsman led their respective races.

While most of those results held strong in subsequent vote tallies, 18-year-old high school senior Jorge Sanchez Bautista steadily gained on La Forte throughout the week — leaving that race too close to call as of Friday evening.

Four of seven PPS school board seats are up for grabs in this election, with just two of those races including an incumbent candidate. PPS board elections are held in May of odd-numbered years, and winners are elected to four-year terms that begin in July.

While the elections are citywide, candidates are running to represent specific geographic districts and they must be residents of those districts. Zone 1 includes South and Southwest Portland, Zone 4 is primarily in North Portland, Zone 5 is primarily in Northeast Portland, and Zone 6 is primarily in Southeast Portland.

ELECTION RESULTS: Find more live updated results on KGW's Elections page

Zone 1

As of Friday evening, Christy Splitt led Ken Cavagnolo in the director Zone 1 race, 82.1% to 16.4%.

Splitt is technically the incumbent, having been appointed to fill a vacant seat on the board earlier this year. She's a PPS parent with background as a high school social studies teacher, legislative work in environmental policy and a role at the Oregon Department of Energy. She has endorsements from sitting board members and the teachers union.

Cavagnolo has a background as an astrophysicist before taking on a business focus, working as an AI executive at a Portland firm.

Zone 4

As of Friday evening, Rashelle Chase-Miller led Herman Greene in the director Zone 4 race, 59.9% to 39.8%.

Greene is the incumbent, having served on the PPS board since 2021. He's a pastor at Abundant Life PDX church and a community advocate, earning a number of endorsements from local and state leaders.

Chase-Miller is a PPS parent and program director of SMART Reading, with a background in early childhood education. She's also earned a number of major endorsements, leaning more progressive than Greene's.

Zone 5

As of Friday evening, Virginia La Forte led Jorge Sanchez Bautista in the director Zone 5 race, 50.3% to 49.4%.

Sanchez Bautista is a McDaniel student and community organizer, having served as a student advisor to the State Board of Education. He's earned a number of big progressive endorsements, particularly on the city council.

La Forte is a PPS parent and marketing and communications specialist, and has served on PPS advisory committees. She's earned endorsements from a number of sitting school board members.

Zone 6

As of Friday evening, Stephanie Engelsman led Rob Galanakis in the director Zone 6 race, 81.6% to 12.1%. Simone Crowe trailed behind with 4.42%.

Galanakis is a software engineer, business owner and PPS parent with endorsements from board member Eddia Wang and Portland City Council Mitch Green.

Engelsman is a public defender and PPS parent with backing from the teachers union and three sitting school board members.

Crowe is an environmental policy advocate with a background in nonprofits, fundraising and communications.
