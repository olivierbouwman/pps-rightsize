Dear Portland,
I’m a parent of a PPS 4th and 6th grader. Small business
owner. Education, climate, and transportation advocate.
I got into this race because Portland Public Schools wasn’t
meeting our kids’ challenges with the urgency, creativity,
and boldness they require.
The crises in our schools won’t be solved with
education policy alone. We expect schools to do more
than ever, in an increasingly difficult environment.
The ultimate cause of our failing schools is the same as
homelessness, the climate crisis, and faltering democracy:
a decades-long ideological assault on civic society, without
an effective response.
The challenges we face are existential.
Schools have a fundamental role to play. Their impact on
population, congestion, zoning, emissions, health, and civic
life is greater than any other comparable entity.
There is a direct line from the achievement gap to freeway
expansion. From falling enrollment to parking mandates.
From school start times to street safety.
School, county, and city leaders must make these
connections, and solve multiple problems at once.
We can, and must, do this, without new taxes or spending
in Salem.
Whoever wins, we all must show up and take part. Each
of us needs to bring our time, treasure, and talent to the
fight.
Every problem we fail to address is another problem we
leave for our kids. Look around; they have enough to deal
with already.
Let your vote, whoever it’s for, be a small step in our
struggle for our schools, city, country, and planet.
