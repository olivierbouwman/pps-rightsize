Jorge is a student, a community organizer, and an
advocate. He’s a proud son of Mexican immigrants, a
supporter of unions, and a voice for working families. If
elected, Jorge will be the FIRST LGBTQ+ person of color
and the youngest school board member in Portland
Public Schools board history. After years of gaining
expertise from his time on the State Board of Education
and his time as a student in Portland schools, Jorge will
work tirelessly to make sure our schools work for all of us–
especially the least heard.
Vote Jorge for Portland Public Schools Zone 5!
As your School Board Member, Jorge will fight for:
• Equitable and Inclusive Schools, where students
receive resources no matter their background–
including changing our lottery school program to serve
students better, advocating for cleaner and safer
schools, and expanding immersion programs.
• Strengthen Community Engagement, with parents,
students, and educators being part of the decision
making process–including visiting schools, hosting
events, and making new connections between the
school board and our communities.
• Financial Stewardship and Accountability, ensuring
that our dollars are going into what we really need–
including prioritizing classroom budgets, putting
students before profits, valuing our teachers and staff
members, and rebuilding trust between Portlanders.
