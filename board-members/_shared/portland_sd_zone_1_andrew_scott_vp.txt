COVID tested our resilience, now it's time to grow thriving schools
Since 2019, I've worked with board and community members to ensure Portland Public Schools become more
racially equitable, financially stable, and academically excellent. We have achieved remarkable outcomes:
• Passed a $1.2 billion bond to renovate Jefferson High School. Provided S60m for the Center for
Black Student Excellence. Allocated hundreds of millions for health and safety improvements
• Implemented new math curriculum with promising early results
• Saw continued increases in graduation rates across all demographic groups. PPS now has some of
the highest graduation rates state-wide
• Adopted comprebensive Climate Crisis Response policy to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and
further climate education
My "to-do" list centers equity, transparency, and accountability:
• Advocating for adequate state funding that allows our schools and students to flourish
• Strengthening the relationships between PPS, Multnomah County, and the City of Portland to
provide wrap around services for vulnerable schools and families, create job pipelines, and
vocational training opportunities
• Growing a PPS culture of appreciation and accountability - appreciation for the amazing teaching
and learning that happens every day and accountability to ensure that every student achieves their
full potential
• Providing for our children's mental, social, and emotional needs, allowing them to grow academically
