Title: PPS to vote on resizing the district
Author: Becca Yanez
Published: 2026-04-14
Fetched: 2026-10-07

PORTLAND, Ore. (KOIN) — Portland Public School leaders are voting on Tuesday on a resolution that could close five to ten schools in 2027.
The district said enrollment levels are declining and that it is facing budget pressures.
Over the weekend at the previous city council meeting, parents expressed frustration with the meeting being “disorganized.”
“It’s pretty disorganized. I’m not entirely sure what the purpose of it is. There was also, ‘enrollment’ was spelled wrong on the slides,” said Courtney Ranstreom, mother of a Buckman Elementary School student. “They’re doing a lot of data collecting, it seems like. Which doesn’t seem to be an efficient use of our time.”
The board is expected to direct the superintendent to launch the rightsizing process.
The meeting starts at six p.m. and is open to the public.
