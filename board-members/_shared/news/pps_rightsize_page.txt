Title: Rightsize, Right Supports, Right for Students - Portland School District
Author: None
Published: 2026-10-01
Fetched: 2026-10-07

Portland Public Schools is continuing opportunities for students, families, staff and community members to learn about rightsizing, ask questions and share their experiences, priorities and perspectives as we plan for the future of our schools.
Next week, PPS will host three Community Voice conversations designed to create space for communities whose experiences and perspectives are important to the rightsizing process.
Native Community Conversation
Tuesday, September 22nd
6:00–8:00 p.m.
Jefferson High School
Hosted in partnership with PPS Indian Education, this conversation is an opportunity for Native and Indigenous students, families and community members to learn more about rightsizing and share what they want district leaders to understand and consider.
View the Native Community Conversation Flyer | RSVP
Multilingual Community Conversation
Wednesday, September 23rd
6:00–8:00 p.m.
Virtual via Zoom (Please RSVP to receive the Zoom link)
This virtual conversation is specifically designed for families who prefer to participate in a language other than English. Interpretation will be available in Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese, Somali and Russian so families can learn about rightsizing, ask questions and share feedback in their preferred language. View the Multilingual Community Conversation Flyer | RSVP
Black/African American Community Conversation
Thursday, September 24th
6:00–8:00 p.m.
Self Enhancement, Inc.
Black and African American students, families and community members are invited to come together for a conversation about rightsizing and the future of PPS. Participants will have an opportunity to hear the latest information, ask questions and share the priorities and experiences they want considered throughout this process. View the Black/African American Community Conversation Flyer | RSVP
Your voice matters.
Rightsizing will affect communities across PPS, and community engagement is an important part of the process. These conversations are opportunities to listen, learn, ask questions and ensure the experiences of our students and families are part of what district leaders hear and consider. We hope you’ll join us and help spread the word to others in your community.
