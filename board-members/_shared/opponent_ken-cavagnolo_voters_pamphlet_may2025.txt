DRASTIC CHANGE CAN’T WAIT!
We cannot ignore declining academic performance,
severely underfunded schools, inverted priorities, and
wasted resources. It’s time for decisive change to ensure
kids receive the education they deserve. As a PPS Board
member…
STUDENTS FIRST
I will compel a relentless focus on teaching and learning
in vibrant classrooms with inspired students motivated to
achieve — high-dose tutoring, early literacy, learning to
learn, smaller classes, more teachers, research-backed
programs.
AGGRESSIVE FUNDING
I will leverage the power of PPS to organize parents,
educators, and students in collaboration with lawmakers to
draft, propose, and push legislation that prioritizes students
— ending “real-time” enrollment funding, killing predatory
corporate tax breaks, reallocating wasted local taxes.
SAVVY SPENDING
I will lead the creation of a simpler, transparent, tracked
budget that allows PPS to redirect wasted funds back
to classrooms and inform creative financing — microbonds, emergency loans, energy performance contracting,
misappropriated taxes.
EDUCATOR EMPOWERMENT
I will create the PPS resolve to cultivate a healthy,
fruitful career environment that uplifts our educators and
recognizes their invaluable contributions — a living wage
initiative, a “Housing for Heroes” program, professional
councils, an educator bill of rights.
