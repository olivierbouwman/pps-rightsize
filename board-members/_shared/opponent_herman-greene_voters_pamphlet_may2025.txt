Equity, Transparency, and Literacy for a Stronger
School System
Portland’s students are full of promise—but systemic
barriers still impact their success. Literacy isn’t just an
academic goal—it’s a matter of justice. Too many students,
especially those of color and from low-income families,
are not reading at grade level. We must act with proven,
evidence-based strategies to close these gaps and give
every child the foundation they deserve.
We need shared academic accountability. Schools,
families, and policymakers must set high expectations,
provide targeted support, and measure progress fairly and
transparently. That same commitment to transparency must
guide the school modernization process. Taxpayer dollars
must be accounted for, and the community must have a
voice in how we invest in learning environments.
Priorities for Portland Public Schools:
• Improve Literacy Outcomes – prioritize early
intervention and effective instruction.
• Increase Transparency in School Modernization –
center community voices in decision-making.
• Address HVAC Failures – ensure safe, healthy learning
environments in elementary and middle schools.
• Strengthen Academic Accountability – support all
students in reaching high standards.
My life’s work has focused on mentorship, restorative
justice, and youth development. I’ve seen the power of
education to transform lives. Leadership means listening,
collaborating, and taking bold action.
I ask for your vote—to lead with compassion, courage,
and urgency.
