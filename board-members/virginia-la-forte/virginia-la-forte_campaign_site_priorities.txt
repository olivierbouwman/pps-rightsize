Priorities
1. Academic Exellence
Virginia is committed to improving reading outcomes, addressing chronic absenteeism, and ensuring safe, modern learning environments—NOW.
2. Equity & Opportunity
Every student deserves access to a high-quality education, regardless of their zip code. Virginia fights to remove barriers and build belonging.
3. Support for Educators
Virginia loves and respects our educators. She believes they deserve classroom supports and a safe, healthy work environment.
4. Real Accountability
Virginia a stickler for transparency and smart implementation of our public funds—planning and follow-through matters.
