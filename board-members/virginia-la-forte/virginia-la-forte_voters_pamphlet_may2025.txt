FIGHTING FOR STUDENTS AND SCHOOLS
Dear Voter,
I’m a fighter, builder, and hard worker. Since age 16—starting
at McDonald’s—I’ve paid my own way: food, rent, college.
My parents struggled financially; thus I bring hustle and
perseverance to everything I do.
I’ll bring urgency and action to address chronic
absenteeism, improve student achievement, secure
better funding, and ensure safe, supportive schools.
I’ll insist on greater financial accountability and rigorous
oversight of district spending.
I ask for your support, Virginia
