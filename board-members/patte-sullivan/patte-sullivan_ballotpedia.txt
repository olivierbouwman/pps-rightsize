Patte Sullivan
Patte Sullivan is a member of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education in Oregon, representing Zone 3. She assumed office on July 1, 2023. Her current term ends on June 30, 2027.
Sullivan ran for election to the Portland Public Schools Board of Education to represent Zone 3 in Oregon. She won in the general election on May 16, 2023.
