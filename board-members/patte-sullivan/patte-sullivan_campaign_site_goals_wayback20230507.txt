My Goals as a Board Member
Support teachers emotionally and financially
Provide safe schools without resorting to police in the schools
Provide social and emotional support for every student
Resist censorship of reading materials and trust teachers to choose age appropriate curriculum and encourage critical thinking
Find support for students that need extra help
Support the arts as well as STEM subjects
Work toward equity and inclusion in our schools. I recognize that bullying is a problem and will work with educators to find a way to make all students feel like they belong
