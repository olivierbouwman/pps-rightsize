*Ex-Officio Community Member Positions

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education Audit Committee includes two ex-officio community member positions.

The time commitment is two to six hours a month. Audit, financial, systems management, and operational experience as well as a commitment to public education is desirable, although not required. If you are interested and would like to apply, please submit a letter of interest and resume to Janise Hansen at jhansen@pps.net.
