Board Committees

The function of Board committees will be to consider actions or issues in detail and to provide policy advice for the Board's action or consideration, as appropriate. Board committees serve in fact-finding and advisory role and will make recommendations to the Board. Committee findings and recommendations will be reported to the full Board in a regular or special meeting of the Board.

All Board committee meetings are publicly noticed and open to the public.
