Portland Public Schools adopts budget for 2026-27 school year after months of debate
PORTLAND Ore. (KPTV) - Oregon’s largest school district has adopted a budget for the 2026-27 school year after months of debate over a $50 million shortfall.
The Portland Public Schools Board adopted the budget after a six-hour session Tuesday. The plan starts July 1.
The seven-member board adopted the budget it approved May 26.
The budget includes the elimination of 320 full-time positions across the district, including positions in the central office and classrooms.
Teachers went to board meetings to urge the board to reconsider districtwide layoffs. During the last week of school, 82 teachers received layoff notices related to the budget reduction process.
The budget includes cuts of 112 full-time positions from schools, 101 positions from specialized programs and 48 positions from transportation and facilities departments.
The adopted budget also includes a roughly 14% reduction in central office staff.
