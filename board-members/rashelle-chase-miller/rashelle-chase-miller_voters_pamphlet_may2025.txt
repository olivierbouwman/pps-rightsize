OPPORTUNITY, ACCESS, ACHIEVEMENT:
Our district faces challenges, but with vision and leadership,
they also present opportunities to create access and
opportunity for students we’ve left behind.
As an educator and working mom to kids at Woodlawn
Elementary and Harriet Tubman Middle School, Rashelle
has dedicated her career to ensuring every child
experiences high-quality education and the support and
resources they need to thrive.
“This moment calls for an experienced leader like
Rashelle who can see the big picture and puts our
students’ success first. As an educator, administrator,
and PPS parent, we need her expertise in literacy,
special education, and school safety now.”
- Michelle DePass and Eddie Wang, Portland Public
School Board
Rashelle will lead with:
• INNOVATION: student-focused solutions that meet
their unique needs post-COVID
• LITERACY: Evidence-based practices that empower all
learners
• ACCOUNTABILITY: Demand better results by fully
funding schools, including special education
• COMMUNITY: safe and inclusive community schools
where kids have their basic needs met through wraparound services
• JUSTICE: eliminate the racial achievement gap
through multi-tiered supports
