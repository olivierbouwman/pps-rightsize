Basic Rights Oregon PAC Announces Candidate Endorsements for May 2025 School Board Election
It’s time to fill out your ballot for the May 2025 Special Election for Oregon School Boards!
Basic Rights Oregon is proud to endorse school board candidates who are committed to supporting inclusivity and affirmation in schools for all students, especially the most marginalized. We have chosen to endorse the list of candidates below, which we considered based on their answers to list of curated questions. We believe these candidates would help make our state schools welcoming environments for ALL students.
Learn more about our PAC and endorsement process here.
A rainbow 🌈 below denotes that a school board candidate is part of the LGBTQ2SIA+ community—and we know every candidate we’ve endorsed will be a strong champion for students.
Thank you for voting!
