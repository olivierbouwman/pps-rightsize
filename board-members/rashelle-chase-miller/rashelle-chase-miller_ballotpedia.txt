Rashelle Chase-Miller
Rashelle Chase-Miller is a member of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education in Oregon, representing Zone 4. She assumed office on July 1, 2025. Her current term ends on June 30, 2029.
Chase-Miller ran for election to the Portland Public Schools Board of Education to represent Zone 4 in Oregon. She won in the general election on May 20, 2025.
