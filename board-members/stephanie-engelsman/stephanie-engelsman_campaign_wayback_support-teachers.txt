My mother was an elementary school teacher. My father-in-law was a special education teacher in Illinois. My mother-in-law was a gifted education teacher in Illinois. My friends are teachers. My neighbors are teachers. I SUPPORT TEACHERS. And when PPS teachers went on strike, my kids and I were on the picket line almost daily.
I believe supporting teachers means (among other things):
** listening to what they need
** paying them well
** capping class sizes
** giving them strong leadership who respects and supports them
Are you a teacher? I'd love to hear from you and I'd love your support. Please reach out at stephanieforourschools@gmail.com.
