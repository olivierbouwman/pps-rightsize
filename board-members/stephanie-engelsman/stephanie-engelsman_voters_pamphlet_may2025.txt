I am running for School Board to build a public school system
that is well-funded, healthy and equitable.
As a public defender of 20+ years, I advocate for our most
vulnerable – youth and families. As a mom to 3, a soccer and
Oregon Battle of the Books coach, and active PPS volunteer,
I know when our kids get support, education and resources,
they thrive. I will fight for this!
Champion for Schools & Students
My priorities:
• A well-funded education, including special education
• Fiscal responsibility: ensure tax dollars are focused
in classrooms; prioritize bond funds to repair, improve
aging schools
• Reading, math supports for students to catch up and
excel
• More art, music!
• Diverse career training, college prep classes
• Protecting marginalized students
• Mental health and counseling in every school
I ask for your support!
