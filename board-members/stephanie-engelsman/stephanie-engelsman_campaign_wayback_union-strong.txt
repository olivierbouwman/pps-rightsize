I have been a union member for most of my career and believe in unions.
Did you know that:
- union members earn 10% more on average that non-union workers
- union members are more likely to have health insurance and pensions
- unions advocate for safer working conditions that prevent injury and illness
- unions promote anti-discrimination measures and advocate for equal pay for equal work
- unions negotiate contracts that guarantee job security and stable schedules
- unions contribute to economic growth and resilience by reducing inequality
I have experienced first-hand all of these benefits and am #unionstrong.
