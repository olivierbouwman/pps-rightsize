Title: A Union Guide to Oregon’s May 20, 2025, special district election
Author: Anna Del Savio
Published: 2025-04-30
Fetched: 2026-10-07

Oregon’s May 20 election is for non-partisan board seats on school districts and other special districts. Nearly all are unpaid volunteer positions. Candidates are seeking public office in special districts responsible for educating Oregon kids, putting out fires in homes and forests, ensuring access to clean water, and more. Fewer than three out of 10 registered voters in Oregon cast ballots in recent special district elections. Candidates in contested races who have endorsements from labor organizations are listed below. See the Legend for acronyms of the endorsing unions.
SCHOOL DISTRICTS
Beaverton School district
37,826 students
Zone 1
Karin Stark worked to get parents on board with the Beaverton Education Association’s asks during contentious contract negotiations that concluded in January. She wants to increase the board’s collaboration with educators. She faces Van Truong and Andrew De Mars for the seat held by outgoing board member Susan Greenberg. NOLC, OSEA 48, Beaverton Education Association (OEA), UFCW 555, Plumbers 290, Ironworkers 29, WFP StarkForBSD.com
Zone 2
Eric McGuire is an OEA member and teacher in Centennial SD who previously taught in the Beaverton School District for 30 years. He’s running against incumbent Karen Pérez-Da Silva, who had union support in previous elections. NOLC, OSEA 48, Beaverton Education Association (OEA), UFCW 555, WFP McGuireforBSD.com
Zone 5
Syed Qasim is a Beaverton parent who has been involved in local task forces on issues like climate action and inclusive housing. He is running against Erin Hatch, a Beaverton parent who serves on the board of a charter school. NOLC, OSEA 48, Beaverton Education Association (OEA), UFCW 555, WFP SyedQasimForBSD.com

16,874 students
Zone 6
Ross Tomlin is a part-time community college forestry teacher who spent 20 years teaching full-time before becoming president of Tillamook Bay Community College. He was appointed to the school board in November 2024. He faces a challenge from Theo Wilhelm, a former middle school teacher. COCLC, Bend Education Association (OEA), WFP RossForEducation.com
Zone 7
Kina Chadwick has served on the school board since December 2022 and works for a nonprofit working to improve access to transgender health care in Central Oregon. Chadwick’s priorities include increasing literacy in early childhood, reducing absenteeism, and making sure all students feel a sense of safety and belonging. Chadwick faces a challenge from Justin Van Patten, a small business owner. COCLC, Bend Education Association (OEA), WFP KinaForOregon.com

Measure 26-259 — Portland Public School Bond Levy
YES Measure 26-259 is a $1.83 billion bond to renovate or replace Cleveland and Ida B. Wells high schools; update and repair elementary and middle school buildings; and upgrade high school athletic and performing arts facilities. The bond would continue modernization work that started with a bond in 2012 and continued with bonds approved in 2017 and 2020. The proposed bond would ramp up as tax payments for previous bonds wind down, so the total bond rate paid by property owners would remain around an estimated $2.50 per $1,000 of assessed value, according to the district. NOLC, AFSCME 189, AFSCME 328, IBEW 48, Ironworkers 29, Portland Association of Teachers (OEA), SEIU 503, UFCW 555, WFP YesPDXschools.com

Mt. Hood Community College
Measure 26-258 — Mt. Hood Community College bond
YES Mt. Hood Community College is seeking a $136 million bond. The college has asked voters to approve bonds six times since 1974, but failed every time. The current bond proposal would add $0.25 per $1,000 of assessed value to property owners’ annual taxes. Funds would upgrade the college’s pool for year-round access; refresh electrical, heating, cooling, and fire safety systems in 50-year-old buildings; modernize security systems and seismic retrofitting; and update classrooms and labs. MHCC Full Time Association (OEA), MHCC Part Time Faculty and Tutor Association (OEA), MHCC Classified Employee Association (OEA), Teamsters Local 223, WFP yesformhcc.com
