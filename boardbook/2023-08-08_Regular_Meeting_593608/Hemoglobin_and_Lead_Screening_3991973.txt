PPS Head Start Health Team incorporates hemoglobin (hgB) and Lead Oregon EPSDT standards to assess risks
that may impact children’s health and development.

Hemoglobin and Lead screening are both included on the PPS Health Exam form for the child’s primary
physician to complete as per Head Start requirements. (See Health Exam Script). Hemoglobin results could also
be obtained from WIC if available, and Lead can be reported from MCHD Lead tes ng clinics.
