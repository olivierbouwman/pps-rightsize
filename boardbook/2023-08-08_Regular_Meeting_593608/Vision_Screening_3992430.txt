 Procedure
 PPS Head Start promotes childhood vision by providing annual screenings with a Plusop x vision screening
 machine. PPS partners with the OHSU Casey Eye ELKS, a local non-proﬁt program that provides vision
 screenings as well as follow up for families who give consent. The ELKS assist in the beginning of the school
 year with vision screenings in classrooms, and reach out to families to oﬀer support for children who had a
 ‘refer’ result.
