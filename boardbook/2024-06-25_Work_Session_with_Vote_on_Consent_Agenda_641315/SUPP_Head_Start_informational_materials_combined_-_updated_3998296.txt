To whom it may concern:

As a part of the school district, we understand that Nutrition Services provides all meals and snacks for
our preschool children. Our Head Start Parent Policy Council has discussed topics related to the food that
is served to our children that we would like to share with you.

Our concerns include:

    ●   The high sugar content in the food given to children. We do not want our children to develop a
        habit of eating foods full of sugar, and as parents, have serious concerns about the potential long
        term health impacts of this.
    ●   The appropriateness of meals for preschool children. There are lots of packaged foods and
        consumption of processed foods, and a lack of culturally–representative meals.
    ●   The high amount of food and packaging waste. We want our children to learn ways to consume
        food sustainably.

In response to these concerns, we ask that you:

    ●   Increase the variety of fresh fruits and vegetables, and substitute processed packaged foods with
        whole foods. (For example: provide real grapes/cranberries instead of craisins or watermelon
        flavored dried cranberries.)
    ●   Share data from a recent nutrient analysis of school meals, including sugar content and any
        concerns voiced by the state agency related to this analysis.
    ●   Share the timeline for the implementation of USDA sugar reductions.
    ●   Share what partnerships the District has with local food programs to help eliminate waste and
        what partnerships are you exploring for further sustainability (for example: Urban Gleaners).

Thank you for your time and consideration.
We welcome you at our next meeting in April to talk about these points.

VISION
Our vision is for children and families to grow, learn, and thrive
in a nurturing bilingual and bicultural environment that fosters
positive Vietnamese cultural identity.




MISSION
Our mission is to support school readiness by providing
students with an authentic learning experience that emphasizes
Vietnamese language and culture through play, stories, songs,
food, and social interactions. We are dedicated to keeping
native Vietnamese families connected to their roots, celebrating
Vietnamese culture, and honoring language through
community partnerships and family support.

INSTRUCTIONAL FRAMEWORK


The PK VDLI Program aligns to the PPS Educational Equity Framework and
Dual Language Immersion Department. The program centers multilingual
learning and dual language instructional best practices for preschoolers.

       Coaches and professional development.

  ●    The classroom Instructional Coach will also have access to additional
       DLI training via site visits to other immersion preschools, learning
       walks, and professional development.

TRANSLATION CONSIDERATIONS
In order to provide a high-quality bicultural and culturally–affirming learning experience for
students and families, the translation of classroom materials from English to Vietnamese is
being done collaboratively and thoughtfully with our Vietnamese staff and partners.

The process of Vietnamese–English translation is nuanced, and according to recent research,
must consider vocabulary, grammatical structures, generational changes, and cultural aspects.
This applies to instructional materials, documents for families, and observation/evaluation.

The teaching team will utilize the expertise and revised materials from K–12 VDLI educators,
and, as needed, can access to translation support from partner organizations. The classroom
educator can also use the PPS DLI Educator Request Process for translation compensation.
