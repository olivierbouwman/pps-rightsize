 3.10.031-P
 Class Size
 Guidelines and
 School staff
 allocations


 The Board directs the superintendent to determine school staff allocation and class
 size guidelines for the district and to communicate that information to all district
 administrators. Class size guidelines and school staff allocations for focus programs,
 focus schools and dual immersion schools shall align with school staff allocations and
 class size guidelines for neighborhood schools.
