Conclusions
1. In the aggregate, Reading and Math student
   performance exceeds the national fall
   benchmarks in all grades.
1. Percentile rank changes show Black and
   Native students performing below their White
   peers, with Black students showing academic
   improvement in almost all grade levels.
1. When MAP RIT score data are disaggregated,
   achievement gaps between student groups
   persist by race and ethnicity and achievement
   changes vary between and among students by
   grade level.
