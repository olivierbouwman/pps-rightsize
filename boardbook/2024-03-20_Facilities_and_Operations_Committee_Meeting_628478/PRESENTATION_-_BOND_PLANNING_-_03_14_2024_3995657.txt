Cost Estimating

For the last 6-8 years, construction cost estimating has not been
very accurate. Estimates have regularly been below market and
experience large discrepancies.

The current cost market continues to be volatile and challenging
to predict.

Projecting future costs, even out a few years, is uncertain.
Projecting costs a decade or more in the future are unreliable.
