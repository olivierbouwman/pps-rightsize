3.50.060-P                Assurance Of Equal Opportunity In                                        B
                          Athletic Programs
                                                                                                   O
        (1) Opportunities for participation in athletic programs shall be equally                  A
            available to boys and girls, and implementation of programs shall proceed
            as rapidly as resources and facilities can be provided.                                R
        (2) Girls may participate on teams designated for boys in any interscholastic
            sport if there is no girl’s team in a comparable sport.                                D
