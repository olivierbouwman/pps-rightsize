Division 22 Standards

     OAR 581-022-2320 Required Instructional Time requires all
     school districts to ensure that at least 92% of all students
     in the district and at least 80% of all students at each
     school operated by the district are scheduled to receive
     annually the minimum hours of instructional time:
         a. Grade 12 - 966 hours;
         b. Grades 9-11 - 990 hours; and
         c. Grades K-8 - 900 hours.
