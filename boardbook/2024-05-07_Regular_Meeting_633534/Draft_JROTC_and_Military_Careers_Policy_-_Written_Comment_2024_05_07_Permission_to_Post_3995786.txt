PPS

As a PPS graduate, I am grateful that my experience did not include JROTC. Students in
Portland Public Schools need a school board that focuses on important tasks that contribute to
students' educations like supporting professional development, ensuring safe facilities,
introducing the new elementary literacy approach and hiring a superintendent, not bringing
militarism into schools. As a child, I was disturbed to learn of mass shootings happening in
schools elsewhere. Two and a half decades later, the problem has only gotten worse and
elsewhere is no longer very far away. Students today don't need more guns or their replicas in
schools. All students deserve real and honest career exploration opportunities that help them
learn about the ways that they can earn high wages while contributing meaningful skills that
build are local community and economy. JROTC is part of a poverty draft that offers an inferior
education to low-income students and students of color. Far from being an opportunity, JROTC
is a diversion of school resources, making it easier to avoid reforming education to address
students’ needs for effective curriculum, small classes, tutoring, accessible extra-curricular
activities and mentoring. Please help keep our kids safe by keeping JROTC out of schools.
