5/6/2024 16:27
Grout and Cleveland
In the past, our family has contributed to foundation fundraising campaigns. We did not
understand the ramifications of those donations. Now that we have experience at multiple
school communities we have come to better understand how the current system is not an
equitable solution to the district's budget challenges. We firmly believe that the entire district
would be better served if we united together to solve the problem of underfunding through
advocacy instead of hosting events that only serve to create less inclusive school cultures. We
fully support this foundation reform proposal. It might not be the magic solution that makes
everyone happy, but we hope it will be an improvement on what is currently practiced. We
welcome a change to the status quo and plan to redirect our donations to the all district fund if
this reform proposal passes.

Klay Arsenault
McDaniel
I fully support the reform of school based fundraising and school foundation fundraising. The
current system is extremely inequitable and will never be able to meaningfully impact schools
that don't have the resources to fundraise. All schools should be fully funded by the district and
the state no matter what the ability of the community to raise funds is.
