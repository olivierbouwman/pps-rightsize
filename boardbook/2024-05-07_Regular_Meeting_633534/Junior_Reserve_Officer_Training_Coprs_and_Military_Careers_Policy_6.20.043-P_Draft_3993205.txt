                    Board Policy                                  6.20.043-P
                    Junior Reserve Officer Training Corps
                    and Military Careers


The District does not prohibit a teacher, counselor or administrator in the
district, at his or her professional discretion, from providing information to a
student about career and educational opportunities in the military, or from
referring a student to a recruitment office.

Schools will provide, at students’ requests, transcripts, records and
references to military offices and institutions to facilitate military
scholarships and appointments or enlistment for military service.

In consultation with and approval of the Superintendent, a high school principal may
establish a Junior Reserve Officer Training Corps program that aligns with PPS
