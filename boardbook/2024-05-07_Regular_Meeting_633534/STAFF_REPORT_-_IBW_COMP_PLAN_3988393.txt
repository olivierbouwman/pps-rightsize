BACKGROUND

Board Resolution 6153 referred the planning and design for the Ida B Wells High School
Modernization project to voters as a part of the 2020 bond.

The Ida B Wells High School Modernization Project Team (Project Team) has worked with
internal stakeholders, external stakeholders, a community Comprehensive Planning Committee
and the internal Ida B Wells Steering Committee to develop a Comprehensive Plan in
conformance with the Comprehensive High School Education Specifications and Design
Guidelines & Standards.

RELATED POLICIES/BEST PRACTICES

The PPS 2017 Comprehensive High School Educational Specifications (2017 Ed Specs), along
with information on current Ida B Wells High School programming, was used as the basis for
programming of the modernized Ida B Wells High School. The Ida B Wells Comprehensive
Plan was also informed by space programming needs of the PPS Climate Crisis Response
Policy.

In addition to using these documents as the foundation for the Ida B Wells program, the Project
Team conducted a rigorous outreach and engagement effort over several months. The Project
Team met with internal and external focus groups and developed a Comprehensive Plan report
for Ida B Wells that presents the desired room requirements, the interrelationships of spaces,
specific room requirements and square footages, and most importantly, represents the core
educational values of PPS.

ANALYSIS OF SITUATION

The existing Ida B Wells High School (IBW), located in the Hillsdale neighborhood of Southwest
Portland and immediately adjacent to Rieke Elementary School, was built in 1956 and is
260,900 square feet. It currently serves approximately 1,600 students. The 26-acre site,
largest of all PPS high school sites, includes a full range of athletic fields, as well as an outdoor
public pool owned and maintained by the City of Portland.

A Conceptual Master Planning effort was undertaken in late 2019 to provide a basis of cost
estimating for future project planning. Based on cost information and other project prioritization,
the Board of Education included $20M for planning and design for the Ida B Wells High School
Modernization project on the 2020 Bond, with the understanding that funds for construction
would need to be included for voter approval in a later bond measure.

As part of the Ida B Wells High School Comprehensive Plan, the Project Team developed a
detailed Area Program Summary that refines the PPS High School Education Specifications so
that the program meets the specific requirements for Ida B Wells High School. The total square
footage is larger than the base 2017 Ed Specs due to three primary reasons:

   ●   Space needs of IBW’s Career Technical Education (CTE) programs - IBW has a robust
       CTE program with CTE classes, such as Wood & Metals, Printmaking, and Health
       Occupations, that require larger instructional spaces
   ●   Lessons learned from previous modernization efforts, including larger Gym size and
       larger Mat Room size (both currently noted as “optional” sizes in 2017 Ed Specs), more
       space for Team Rooms for athletics, increased field storage space for athletics/PE, and
       additional building maintenance and operations support spaces
   ●   Spaces related to PPS’ resiliency goals, including additional space needed for electrified
       versus fossil fuel equipment, and storage related to emergency shelter needs

In addition to the larger square footage, the IBW Comprehensive Plan addresses the unique
situation of the City-owned, outdoor public pool on the PPS-owned IBW site. In accordance
with direction from the Board of Education Facilities & Operations Committee at the February
28, 2024, meeting, the IBW Comprehensive Plan leaves the existing pool in place to continue
operating as it has been. The restrooms and lockers that currently serve the pool will be
demolished as they are within the existing school building that is also intended for demolition. A
new restroom and locker facility will be provided as part of the Ida B Wells High School
Modernization project.

The Facilities & Operations Committee also discussed two potential site layout options, at the
March 20, 2024, meeting. One layout retained the east-west oriented track and field, while the
second layout re-oriented the track and field to north-south. While there were advantages and
disadvantages to both options, the loose consensus by Board Committee members was
towards the site layout option which is now included in the attached IBW Comprehensive Plan.

FISCAL IMPACT

The total project budget identified in the 2020 Bond materials for the planning and design for the
Ida B Wells High School Modernization project was $20,000,000. While rough cost estimates

The Project Team utilized professional cost estimators to establish a project budget. Based on

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

A community engagement consultant conducted research and analysis to inform and establish a
framework for the community-based engagement component of the modernization planning
process. Individual interviews and focus groups with the consultant provided actionable
information to the Project Team regarding the overall IBW community landscape, demographic
insights, and key audience engagement considerations; as well as identified gaps, needs and
opportunities related to community engagement to inform the design, approach, recruitment,
and facilitation of the community engagement sessions. The sessions were crafted with the goal
of ensuring equity-informed engagement of often marginalized community voices. Along with
other, more focused public engagement sessions, PPS held three public community design
workshops that were well attended in October 2023, December 2023 and February 2024.

In addition, a Comprehensive Planning Committee (CPC) met on six separate occasions
between October 2023 and March 2024 to review, discuss and recommend next steps for the
comprehensive plan of the modernization project. The CPC comprises representatives from
many stakeholder groups including students, parents, alumni and parents from feeder schools,
as well as teachers, coaches and staff. CPC members were specifically chosen to represent as
many stakeholder groups as possible in the Ida B Wells community and vicinity. This committee
also toured Lincoln High School as an example of a new high school in the district.

TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION / EVALUATION

If the proposed IBW Comprehensive Plan is approved, the current Architecture/Engineering
project team will continue forward with building and site design. This will take place in
coordination with ongoing community engagement, including a community-based Design
Advisory Group as well as open houses, small focus groups, and other engagement activities.
Design is expected to complete in winter 2025.

In order for construction to occur on the Ida B Wells High School Modernization project, funding
will need to be secured through a future bond measure. If funding is approved and construction
is able to commence in early 2026 per the schedule provided in the attached IBW

CONNECTION TO BOARD GOALS

The new modernized Ida B Wells High School will provide each student with an equitable,
individualized, high-quality learning experience and the tools to reach their full potential within
an environment that is safe, healthy, and joyful. The new Ida B Wells High School will embody
PPS’s Mission, Vision and Values, and its Climate Policy, to strengthen the future of
its students and its communities.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff requests approval of the proposed Ida B Wells High School Comprehensive Plan.
