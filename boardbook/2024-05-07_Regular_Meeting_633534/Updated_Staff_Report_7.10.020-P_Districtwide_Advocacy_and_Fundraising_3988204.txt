BACKGROUND

The District’s policy Parent Groups and the Schools 7.10.020-P was last revised in 2002. The
Board Policy Committee and community members have had ongoing discussions for several
years regarding governance and practices around school-based fundraising to pay for staff.

The proposed revised policy is a rewrite of the existing policy and changes the existing model of
local school foundation policies and relies on a district-wide foundation model.

On March 18, 2024, the Policy Committee recommended this revised policy go to the full Board
for a First Reading. The policy had a First Reading at the April 2, 2024, Board meeting. The
Policy Committee met on April 11, 2024, and recommended deleting one sentence from the
policy related to the appointment of Board members of the District foundation, which is reflected
in the posted policy. The Committee also received public comment on the proposed policy at
that meeting. The Policy Committee also received public comment on the proposed policy at its
April 29, 2024, meeting.


RELATED POLICIES/BEST PRACTICES

District Foundation 7.10.030-P.


ANALYSIS OF SITUATION

The proposed revised policy:

      ●   Affirms the important role of school communities in supporting our schools, including
          advocacy and fundraising.
      ●   Affirms an inclusive districtwide foundation serves as districtwide entity and fiscal agent
          for local school foundations.
                                                                                                        1
   ●   Effective July 1, 2024, provides that donations to fund staff positions may be collected
       only by a districtwide foundation. A formula for distribution of funding to individual
       schools shall be developed by a Parent Advisory Committee and approved by the Board.
   ●   Provides for annual reporting to the Board regarding foundation donations, expenditures,
       and major projects.
   ●   Provides requirements for how fundraising groups inclusively engage with school
       communities.
   ●   Requires that the District foundation shall have a board of directors be of a sufficient size
       and diversity of experience.

Legal concerns referenced at the April 2, 2024, meeting have been addressed.

FISCAL IMPACT

Shifting Local School Foundations from the current system to a single, combined fund account
will have an unknown fiscal impact. The amount contributed by Local School Foundations in
2022-23 was $2.5 million (with $1,747,000 allocated to the schools that fundraised + $729,000
to PPS Parent Fund Awards). The fiscal impact over the short and long term will depend on the
size and timing of contributions of time, money, and other resources to a centralized fundraising
model.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

There has been significant community input on this policy issue over several years.


TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION/EVALUATION and COMMUNICATION PLAN

Beginning July 1, 2024, donations for staff positions will be designated to the District-wide
foundation. Other provisions of the policy take effect upon adoption.
