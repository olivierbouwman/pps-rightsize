As a PPS parent I strongly support changing the way school foundations collect and
distribute fundraising monies. The current model is inequitable, and out of step with
many school districts across the nation (and in our region) that do not allow schools to
privately fundraise for FTE at their own school. The State of Oregon must fully fund K-
12 education, but even though they are utterly failing at doing so that doesn't justify an
inequitable practice that privileges wealthy school communities over others.
