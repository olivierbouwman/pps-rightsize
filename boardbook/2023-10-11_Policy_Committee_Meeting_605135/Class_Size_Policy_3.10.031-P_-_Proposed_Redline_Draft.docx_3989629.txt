 3.10.031-P
 Class Size
 Guidelines and
 School staff
 allocations
Draft 9/12/23




 The Board directs the superintendent to determine school staff allocation and class
 size guidelines for the district and to communicate that information to all district
 administrators. Class size guidelines and school staff allocations for focus option
 schools and dual-immersion schools and programs shall align with school staff
 allocations and class size guidelines for neighborhood schools.
