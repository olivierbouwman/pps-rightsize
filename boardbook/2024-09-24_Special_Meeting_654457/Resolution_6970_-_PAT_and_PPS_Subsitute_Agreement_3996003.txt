                                       RESOLUTION No. 6970

2024-2026 Agreement between Portland Association of Teachers/Substitute Teachers and School District
                              No. 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon

                                            RESOLUTION

The Superintendent is authorized and directed to execute the 2024-2026 Agreement between the
Portland Association of Teachers, representing Substitute Teachers, and School District No. 1J,
