PROJECT NOTES
Progress:
 - ADA signage installation has been completed. Some wall repairs are still required and will begin in January.
 - Classroom door hardware (Hardware Upgrades 5999) have been replaced at all sites as of October 2023, excluding hazmat doors (approx 250 doors at 35 sites). New doors have been ordered
for 4 sites and are expected to begin installation mid-January, with ordering and installation continuing through the end of August. All hazmat doors will be replaced before the beginning of the
2024/25 school year.
 - Phase 1 of ADA and SPED upgrades at 38 sites (4 construction packages) are in progress. Packages 1A, 1B & 1C are expected to run long due to a permitting delay at 5 sites. Permits are still
under review for unforeseen requirement of backflow protection at the water supply Chapman, Ockley Green, Mt Tabor, George & Vestal and are expected to be issued at the beginning of
December. Work at sites not mentioned is expected to complete on time.
- Phase 2 of ADA and SPED upgrades at 41 sites (4 construction packages) are in progress and at 90% DD. SD estimates came in higher than anticipated, DD estimates are currently being
prepared.

Accomplishments:
 - ADA signage installation is complete.
 - Door hardware upgrades are compete, excluding hazmat doors which are expected to be completed by end of August 2024.
- ADA & SPED Upgrades at 38 sites is approximately 40% complete. 33 of these sites are on schedule to be complete by the end of January.

Risks:
 - Hardware- approximately 250 doors at 33 sites need to be replaced due to hazardous materials. Costs and installation timeline have not yet been established. These replacements could lead to
schedule and/or budget risks.
 - RPBA requirements at 5 sites will add cost and time to 3 of the 4 packages currently under construction.
 - All 4 construction package bids for phase 1 came in higher than expected, so there is some risk that the same will happen for phase 2 packages.
