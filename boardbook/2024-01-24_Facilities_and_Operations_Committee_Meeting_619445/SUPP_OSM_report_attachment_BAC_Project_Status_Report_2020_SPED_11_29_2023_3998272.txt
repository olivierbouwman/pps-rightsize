PROJECT NOTES
Progress:
 - Furniture for SPED focus classrooms and learning centers has been installed in 43 classrooms at 17 sites. Phased installation across all District SPED classrooms and learning center continues
on schedule at the remaining sites, with 31 classrooms at 15 sites before the end of the calendar year. All remaining classrooms are on schedule to be installed before the start of the 2024/25
school year.
 - Construction of Phase 1 ADA and SPED upgrades at 38 sites (4 construction packages) is in progress. These projects include scope of work for both the ADA funding and the SPED funding,
since the two scopes include aligning construction work. Work for SPED includes SPED-specific restroom upgrades in Intensive Skills classrooms. Packages 1A, 1B & 1C are expected to run long
due to a permitting delay at 5 sites. Permits are still under review for unforeseen requirement of backflow protection at the water supply Chapman, Ockley Green, Mt Tabor, George & Vestal and
are expected to be issued at the beginning of December. Work at sites not mentioned is expected to complete on time.
- Design of Phase 2 ADA and SPED upgrades at 41 sites (4 construction packages) is in progress and at 90% DD. SD estimates came in higher than anticipated, DD estimates are currently being
prepared.

Accomplishments:
- Furniture for SPED focus classrooms and learning centers has been installed in 43 classrooms at 17 sites.

Risks:
- Schedule for furniture installation is tight across multiple sites.
- RPBA requirements at 5 sites will add cost and time to 3 of the 4 packages currently under construction.
- All 4 construction package bids for phase 1 came in highter than expected, so there is some risk that the same will happen for phase 2 packages.
