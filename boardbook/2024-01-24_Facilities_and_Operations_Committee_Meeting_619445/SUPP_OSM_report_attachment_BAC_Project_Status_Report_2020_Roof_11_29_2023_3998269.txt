PROJECT NOTES
Progress:
 - Duniway Phase 2, Glencoe, Markham, Meek, MLC, Richmond, Rieke, Skyline, and Winterhaven were substantially or fully complete as of November 2023 reporting. Some
minor punchlist or follow-up items will continue on a few of these.
 - Minor PBOT-related work remains to be completed at Duniway, due to delays from PBOT in issuing the permit. Permit issuance is now expected in spring 2024, with work
to be performed after school-year end in June 2024.
 - A second summer of Vernon (scope delayed due to equipment procurement issues) and the previously planned second summer of West Sylvan will commence mid-June
2024 and complete in fall 2024.

Accomplishments:
- Early spring bidding helped project schedules and completion of work on time.
- Significant historical restoration work completed at Duniway with replacement of balustrade, clay tile roofing, and copper trim.

Risks:
 - There is risk for a shortened summer work schedule in summer 2024 due to potential makeup time requirements as a result of teacher's strike. This would affect second-
summer work at Vernon and West Sylvan.

- Budget Impacts have been noted in the scorecard above because all summer 2023 roof projects came in substantially over budget, thus limiting the number of overall
projects that will be able to be completed with 2020 Roof funds.
