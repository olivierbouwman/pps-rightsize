                                  RESOLUTION No. 7011

                Resolution to Support Katrina Doughty for the Oregon School
                     Board Association Board of Directors Position 17

                                        RECITALS

A. Portland Public Schools is a member of the Oregon School Boards Association.

B. The Oregon School Boards Association requests the Portland Public Schools Board of
   Education cast its vote for OSBA governance positions.

                                        RESOLVED

Portland Public Schools Board of Education supports Katrina Doughty for the OSBA Board of
Directors Position 17.
