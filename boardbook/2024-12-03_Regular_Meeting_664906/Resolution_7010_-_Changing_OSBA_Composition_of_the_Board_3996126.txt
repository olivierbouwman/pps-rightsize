                                       RESOLUTION No. 7010

      Resolution to Support Amending the Oregon School Boards Association’s Bylaws Relating to
                               Composition of the Board of Directors

                                             RECITALS

A. Portland Public Schools is a member of the Oregon School Boards Association.

B. The Oregon School Boards Association requests the Portland Public Schools Board of Education
   cast its vote for the Resolution to Amend Oregon School Boards Association’s Bylaws Relating to
   Composition of the Board of Directors.

                                             RESOLVED

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education supports the Resolution to Amend Oregon School
Boards Association’s Bylaws Relating to Composition of the Board of Directors.
