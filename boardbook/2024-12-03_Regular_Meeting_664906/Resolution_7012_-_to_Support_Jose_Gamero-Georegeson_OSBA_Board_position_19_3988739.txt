                                 RESOLUTION No. 7012

               Resolution to Support Jose Gamero-Georgeson for the Oregon
                 School Board Association Board of Directors Position 19

                                        RECITALS

A. Portland Public Schools is a member of the Oregon School Boards Association.

B. The Oregon School Boards Association requests the Portland Public Schools Board of
   Education cast its vote for OSBA governance positions.

                                       RESOLVED

Portland Public Schools Board of Education supports Jose Gamero-Georegeson for the OSBA
Board of Directors Position 19.
