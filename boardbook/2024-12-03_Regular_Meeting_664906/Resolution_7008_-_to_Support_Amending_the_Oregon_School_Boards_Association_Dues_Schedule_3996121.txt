                                       Resolution No. 7008

        Resolution to Support Amending the Oregon School Boards Association Dues Schedule

                                            RECITALS

   A. Portland Public Schools is a member of the Oregon School Boards Association.

   B. The Oregon School Boards Association requests the Portland Public Schools Board of Education
      cast its vote for the Resolution to Amend the OSBA Dues Schedule.

                                           RESOLVED

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education supports the Resolution to Amend the OSBA Dues
Schedule.
