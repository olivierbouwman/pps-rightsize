                                       Resolution No. 7009

                       Resolution to Support Amending the OSBA 2023 Bylaws

                                            RECITALS

A. Portland Public Schools is a member of the Oregon School Boards Association.

B. The Oregon School Boards Association requests the Portland Public Schools Board of Education
   cast its vote for the Resolution to Amend the OSBA 2023 Bylaws.

                                            RESOLVED

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education supports the Resolution to Amend the OSBA 2023
Bylaws.
