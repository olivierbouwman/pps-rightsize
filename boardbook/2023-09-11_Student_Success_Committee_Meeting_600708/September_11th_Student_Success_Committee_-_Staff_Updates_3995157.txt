                PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
Updates About JROTC Research
 ●   The 8th Brigade informed that PPS can apply if interested in adding
     JROTC to one or more of our schools.

 ●   If our application is approved for the 2024-2025 school year, we will
     receive the necessary funding to begin the program. The funding will
     cover the cost of uniforms and will pay for the qualiﬁed instructor.

 ●   We were informed that several schools in Oregon, including Madras
     High School, are unable to staff their JROTC because they are unable
     to ﬁnd enough qualiﬁed instructors.
