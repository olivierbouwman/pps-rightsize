                                        STAFF REPORT

Date:          September 26, 2023

To:            School Board

From:          Mary Kane, Senior Legal Counsel

Subject:       Revision to Naming School District Property 2.20.010-P



BACKGROUND

This policy was last amended in 2018. The proposed revisions came before the Board’s Policy
Committee on September 18, 2023, for discussion. Changes in the policy include shifting
authorization for school name change decisions to the Board, as well as clarifying language
about renaming to address racial and other systemic discrimination.

FISCAL IMPACT

There may be a fiscal impact as this policy is put into practice. Fiscal impact is addressed in the
policy.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

There was no community engagement other than two public Policy Committee meetings.

TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION / EVALUATION

This will be implemented upon passage by the Board.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends the Board adopt this amended policy.
