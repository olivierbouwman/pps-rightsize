The Center for Black Student Excellence Presents: The Space Matters Series

Purpose: During this experiential series, we’ll re-establish a shared sense of
community, ground ourselves in the local history that makes the Albina
neighborhood so signiﬁcant, collectively problem solve around new challenges
related to capital projects in the area including the Jeﬀerson High School
modernization and Harriet Tubman Middle School relocation, deﬁne logistical
transitions for students, identify the home of the new Center for Black Student
Excellence, and end with reimagining a pathway that braids the educational
experience of our students together in the heart of historic Black Portland!
