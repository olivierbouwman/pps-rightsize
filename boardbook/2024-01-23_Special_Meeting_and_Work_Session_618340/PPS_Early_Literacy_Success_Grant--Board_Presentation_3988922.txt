OR Early Literacy Success Initiative (HB 3198)
In 2023, the OR Legislature established early literacy
as a top priority. In creating the Early Literacy
Success Initiative, they identiﬁed four goals:
1. Increase early literacy for children from birth to
third grade;
2. Reduce literacy academic disparities for student
groups that have historically experienced academic
disparities;
3. Increase support to parents and guardians to
enable them to be partners in the development of
their children’s literacy skills and knowledge; and
4. Increase access to early literacy learning through
support that is research-aligned, culturally
responsive, student-centered and
family-centered.
