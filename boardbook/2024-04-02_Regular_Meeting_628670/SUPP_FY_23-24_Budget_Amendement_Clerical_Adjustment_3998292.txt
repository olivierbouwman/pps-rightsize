Subject:      Minor Clerical Error on Attachment A of Budget Amendment No. 1


This memo is to inform you of a minor clerical correction to the Attachment A for Resolution No.
6813, Amendment No. 1 to the Fiscal Year 2023-24 Budget for School District No. 1J,
Multnomah County, Oregon approved by the Board on January 9, 2024.

On page 2 of Attachment A, under “600 - Internal Service Funds”, the Adjustment column
(middle column) correctly reflects the transfer of $1 Million from Contingency to Support
Services. However, in the Amended Budget column (right column), the numbers for both
Support Services and Contingency lines were not updated to reflect the shift.

We are submitting an updated Attachment A to reflect this minor change with the correct totals
in the Amended Budget column. This fix does not change the bottom line of $8,998,000.
