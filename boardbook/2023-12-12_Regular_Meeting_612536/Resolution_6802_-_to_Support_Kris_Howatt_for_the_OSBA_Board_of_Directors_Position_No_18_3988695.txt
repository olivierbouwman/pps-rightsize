                                      RESOLUTION No. 6802

              Resolution to Support Kris Howatt for the Oregon School Boards Association
                                Board of Directors Position Number 18

                                             RECITALS

A.   Portland Public Schools is a member of the Oregon School Boards Association.

B.   The Oregon School Boards Association requests the Portland Public Schools Board of Education to
     cast its vote for OSBA governance positions.

                                             RESOLVED

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education supports Kris Howatt for the OSBA Board of Director
position 18.
