                                         STAFF REPORT
Date:            December 12, 2023

To:              Board of Education

From:            Dan Jung, Chief Operating Officer

Subject:         Jefferson High School Modernization Project


On December 6, 2023 District Staff provided the Facilities & Operations (F&O) Committee with a
Jefferson High School Modernization Project update. Due to the combination of schedule risks,
projected cost overruns and community concerns, staff recommended the project design be re-
evaluated to ensure students will remain on campus during construction and strive to find feasible
alternative design options. The attached Board Resolution reflects the feedback discussion and
feedback received from the F&O Committee. Of particular note, committee members addressed
questions about target enrollment by confirming the Board’s prior direction that the project should
align to educational specifications for a comprehensive high school.
