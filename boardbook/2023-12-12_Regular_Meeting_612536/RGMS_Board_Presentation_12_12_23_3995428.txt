    School Board Presentation
Vision Statement


    Robert Gray Middle School believes every student is an
 essential stakeholder and collaborator in their academic and
social-emotional growth and that together we can build a better
              world in which we can all succeed.
Mission Statement


   RGMS partners with families and the community to
 prepare students for success in the 21st century by using
an equity lens and engaging in relevant opportunities that
 center youth voice and promote academic, physical, and
                 social-emotional growth.
SCIP Goal #3:

By the Spring of 2026, the RGMS SCIP Cohort will report an increase
of 10% (students who Agree and Strongly Agree) in the Successful
Schools Survey Category of “Sense of Belonging” and the following
statements from the RGMS School Climate Survey:
 ● “Students have a voice at our school,”
 ● “Student Council and Leadership make the school a better place,”
 ● “Adults listen to students' ideas about how to make the school a
    better place.”
