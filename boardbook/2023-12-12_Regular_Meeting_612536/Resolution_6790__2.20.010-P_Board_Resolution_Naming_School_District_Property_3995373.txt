                                        RESOLUTION No. 6790

               Resolution to Adopt Revised Naming School District Property 2.20.010-P

                                              RECITALS

A. On August 7, August 28, and September 18 2023, the Board Policy Committee reviewed
   and considered the proposed revisions of the Naming School District Property 2.20.010-P
   policy.

B. On October 10, 2023, the Board presented the first reading of the revised Naming School
   District Property policy.

C. Pursuant to District policy, the public comment was open for at least 21 days, and there was no
   public comment received during the comment period.

                                            RESOLUTION

    The Board hereby adopts the revised Naming School District Property 2.20.010-P policy and
    instructs the Superintendent to amend any relevant administrative directives to conform to this
    adopted policy.
