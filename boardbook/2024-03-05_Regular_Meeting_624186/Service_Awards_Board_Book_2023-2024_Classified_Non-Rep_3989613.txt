to all of the following employees for their devoted service to the students, families, and staff of Portland Public
Schools. Your dedication and service to the Portland community are not taken for granted. From July 1, 2023
through June 30, 2024, years of service are honored in five-year increments ranging from five years to forty-five
years. All service award recipients will receive a lanyard signifying their years of service.
