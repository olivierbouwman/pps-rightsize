                 Who was Jason Lee?




- First Protestant Christian missionary to come to the Oregon Territory
- His goal was to convert Native Americans to Christianity and assimilate
  them to western culture
- He had very harmful views of the indigenous population, and wanted
  Oregon to be a white civilization
- He created a school to “educate and civilize” the indigenous children in
  a Christian manner. There were 14 students the first year - 7 died and
  5 ran away.
- He failed to value Native American tribes’ culture, society, & practices
- He founded an institute that eventually would become Willamette
  University, which has recently acknowledged complicated ties to its
  founder and shifted its mission and commitment to support and
  engage its Indigenous students.
