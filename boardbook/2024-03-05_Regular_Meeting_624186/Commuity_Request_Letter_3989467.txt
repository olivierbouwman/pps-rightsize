                                                                              5/10/2023




Dear Principal Alma Velázquez,

Representing the school community—staff, students, alumni, and families of Jason Lee
Elementary—we are asking you to share our thoughts with the PPS District
Administration with the hope that we can begin the process to change the name of our
school. We are aware that this is a process that you and many others have inquired
about through the years, and now we have the traction to create change.

Our school’s namesake, Jason Lee, was a known conversion missionary, having removed
Kalapuya Indians from the Willamette plains and forcing upon them an extensive reeducation
pedagogy. His beliefs and actions stand in direct conflict with our school’s and our
district’s commitment to racial equity and social justice.

More than 75 students, families and staff have signed a petition asking that we rename
the school. We believe that we must change the name of our school and that we must
begin this important process now. We are prepared to weave this process into school
curricula, and we are committed to keeping the community at the heart of this process.

Looking forward to discussing next steps.

In solidarity,

Brittni Jones and Jenn Sonntag
Representing the parents of JLE students
