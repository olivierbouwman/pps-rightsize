Subject:       Background Information re. Audit Finding


We wanted to share some background information regarding a finding in this year's Annual
Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR). The Board’s Audit Committee also held a thorough
discussion of the ACFR on December 19 including discussing this finding and its remedies,
which are being implemented.

The finding pertains to two revenue transactions the auditors noted as misclassified. The first
transaction involved $9.3 million that the District received in June 2022 through the federal
Emergency Connectivity Fund grant. These funds allowed the district to cover purchases of
Chromebooks and hotspots that had initially been funded through a combination of other grants
and bond funds. When the ECF grant was awarded, the revenue was recorded in a Special
Revenue fund (Fund 205), and approved expenditures were reclassified accordingly. However,
when shifting the expenditures, revenue was also inadvertently coded in a capital projects fund
(Fund 458), which effectively duplicated revenue recording. Due to the complexity of this
transaction spanning two fiscal years, staff shortages/outages, and necessary consultation with
internal (OTIS and Grant Accounting) and external (Federal ECF grant personnel) partners, this
duplication of revenue was not initially caught on review.

The second transaction involved $120M that PPS received in December 2022 from the State of
Oregon for relocation/replacement of Harriet Tubman MS. The full amount of the grant was
inadvertently recorded as revenue when received, but should have been recorded as a liability
(deferred revenue) until earned. Revenue would be "earned" as progress advanced on the
relocation/replacement. Due to the complexity of the transaction, which included establishing a
new fund and project ID for tracking/reporting, as well as difficulty getting details regarding the
agreement, the recognition of revenue/deferred revenue was not initially caught on review.

These issues are addressed in a single audit finding for which corrective actions have been
identified, and the appropriate adjustments have been reflected in the financial statements.
