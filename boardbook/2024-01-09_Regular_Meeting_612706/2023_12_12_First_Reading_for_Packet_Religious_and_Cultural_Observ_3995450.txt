Subject:       Religious and Cultural Observances



BACKGROUND

The District has long wrestled with the need to provide space to allow students and staff to
exercise their religious and cultural beliefs while balancing its constitutional duty to preserve
schools from religious entanglement. In the last few years, the District has developed a school
calendar that includes significant dates for building staff to be aware of when scheduling
important school events such as parent teacher conferences. The School Board’s policy
committee continued this exploration through the development of a policy to provide clearer
guidance to school staff on expectations for accommodations for students. The policy
committee discussed this policy on September 18, 2023, October 11, 2023, and finally on
November 30, 2023 where they determined to send it to the full Board for consideration.


RELATED POLICIES/BEST PRACTICES

It is best practice to develop policies that are inclusive and welcoming to students and families.

FISCAL IMPACT

There is no fiscal impact.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT (IF APPLICABLE)


Students and families have provided input in various ways, including through public comment at
Board meetings and in the Board’s policy committee meetings.

cultural practices or not adopt this policy and allow for the continued informal practice of notice
provided to buildings through use of the District calendar.

                               Religious and Cultural Observances

We respect and protect the constitutional rights of our students, families, and employees to
practice and observe their religion or to practice no religion at all. We also respect and protect
our students, families, and employees’ rich and diverse cultural observances and practices. At
all times, we seek to balance those important individual rights with the constitutional
requirement that PPS, as a public school district, may not favor or promote a particular religion
over another. And at all times, we encourage all students, families, and staff to show respect,
tolerance, and curiosity about religious and cultural practices that differ from their own. These
differences and our acceptance of them make our school community an inclusive and
welcoming community in which all students can authentically thrive.

                         Excused Absences & Other Accommodations

Students and families may request accommodations to enable them to observe their religious
and cultural practices. Those accommodations may include excused absences for religious
instruction or holiday observances. Requests for absences or other accommodations should be
in writing from a student’s parent/guardian directed to a teacher and/or principal at the student’s
school.

Classes. Students may be excused from classes for their individual religious or cultural
practices without penalty. Students who have missed an assignment or assessment must be
given a reasonable opportunity to make up that test or assessment. Make-up opportunities will
not be required of a student on the school day immediately after a student is absent from school
to observe a religious or cultural holiday.

Co-Curricular and Extracurricular activities. Students may also be excused from
co-curricular and extracurricular activities for their individual religious or cultural practices
without penalty.

            Identification of Major Religious Holidays and Cultural Observances

As part of the annual school calendar creation and adoption process, the Superintendent shall
identify major religious and cultural holidays in our community using legally acceptable methods
to do so. The District and each of its schools should avoid scheduling grade-, school- or
district-wide events (e.g., Back-to-School nights, all-school assemblies, parent-teacher
conferences, School Board meetings) on those dates. Such events can be scheduled on major
religious holidays only if such scheduling is reasonably necessary to carry out the proper
functioning of a school program, course of study, or to avoid an unreasonable burden on other
students; or if such scheduling is outside the control of District employees. Staff will inform
students and parents of plans as far in advance as possible so that conflicts with religious or
cultural observances can be avoided if possible and otherwise accommodated if not.
                                Non-District-Sponsored Events

When school or District events are scheduled and controlled by organizations other than the
District (e.g., OSAA, Outdoor School, national testing organizations), the District shall advocate
with those organizations that such events are not held on major religious or cultural holidays.
