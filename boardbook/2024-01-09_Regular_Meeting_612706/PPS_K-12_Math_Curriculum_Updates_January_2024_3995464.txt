How We Monitor and Follow Up on Math
Curriculum Implementation
●   School Continuous Improvement Plans provide a roadmap to increased student achievement. All schools have updated
    plans with implementation of new curriculum as a high priority. Written feedback given by SDS’s on all SCIPs.
●   Principals must present progress on SCIPs in academic conferences three times per year to their cohort, their SDS and
    senior leadership.
●   Principals meet with senior directors once per week as a group in cohort and individually at least once every two weeks
    depending on school performance. Consistent visit template used highlighting instructional priorities and observing
    classrooms together.
●   By the end of the 2023-24 school year, all teachers will have at least 3-5 informal observations (classroom walkthrough)
    where they are given a written praise, probing question, and action step within 24 hours of the observation. Consistent
    informal classroom observation template is utilized highlighting observations on instructional priorities.
●   Quarterly reviews with SDS, instructional coach and ILT to discuss and provide feedback on progress on instructional
    priorities.
●   Instructional Rounds problem of practice (GLSA and curriculum adoption)…last year 78 schools were visited. Secondary
    school themes.
