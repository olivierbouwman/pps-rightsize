Context

     ○   The audit is now in its final stages.

General Funds (Fund 100): Requirements/Expenditures

+$7.8M    Instruction requirements for 23-24 increased to include contractual agreement with PAT. Costs for the
          next two years will increase significantly with implementation of higher COLAs, planning time,
          behavioral and mental health supports, and adjustments to substitute, overage, and extended hours
          costs. Cost of health premiums have been adjusted.

+$3.5M    Support Services requirements increased to include proposed increases for other labor partners (e.g.,
          increasing minimum hourly wage). These requirements also include adjustments to fund
          transportation and maintenance costs. Cost of health premiums have been adjusted.

+$3.8M    Contingency requirements increased. The District is planning for $10M in spending restrictions and
          reductions in 23-24 to support increased costs resulting from bargaining as well as evaluating possible
          savings from vacancies/hiring freezes.

Early Literacy Grant
 Background
  ● This is a new grant designed to support literacy development for our youngest learners.
  ● CBRC has recommended increased support for early literacy.

 Preliminary Proposal
  ● Additional Reading Intervention Specialists
  ● Expand Science of Reading aligned resources, training and teacher professional capacity
  ● Summer 2024 Learning Acceleration
  ● High Dosage Tutoring
  ● Supports for multilingual, emergent bilingual, and special education students


 Total: $3.2 million

Integrated Grant
 Background
  ● PPS’s Integrated Grant allocations within the Student Investment Account and M98 were adjusted per ODE due
       to changes in state resourcing. The state resources that fund these two grants had shifts in overall revenue,
       meaning all school districts across Oregon saw changes to these allocations for FY24.

 Preliminary Proposal
   ● Lessen impact of High School Success (M98) funding decrease. SIA absorbed $500k; reductions within the High
       School Success budget are in contracts/supplies/materials.
   ● School-based coordinators to address student out-of-school learning barriers (Community Schools) - 3 FTE
       through December 2023
   ● Summer programming 2024
   ● Additional Comprehensive School Psychologists - 3 FTE
   ● Dual Language Immersion/English Language Development focused staff to support the programmatic,
       assessment, and wrap-around family needs of our multilingual learners - 4 FTE
   ● Instructional specialists at new Comprehensive School Improvement (CSI)-designated schools - 3 FTE (may
       increase as we learn more about designations)

 Total: $5.5 million

CBRC Feedback - Key Takeaways
 Strengths & Opportunities
 Student Enrollment Pilot Program: Members appreciated and supported the proactive enrollment studies that the
 district is pursuing.

 Continued Investment in Learning Acceleration: Members re-emphasized the CBRC recommendation to transition
 ESSER funded learning acceleration specialists to ongoing funding and stated their support of transitioning the Director
 of Learning Acceleration role to ongoing funding.

 Areas of Concern & Further Refinement
 Early Literacy Investments: Members encouraged further investment in FTE for early literacy and highlighted a need for
 more educational assistants in the early grades.

 Social-Emotional Investments: Members described a need for more social-emotional learning investments, particularly
 in middle grades, and the need for more counselors.

 Reserves
 Members expressed the need to balance both the importance of building the reserves for long-term planning as well
 as the need to make immediate investments, particularly in student-facing FTE.
