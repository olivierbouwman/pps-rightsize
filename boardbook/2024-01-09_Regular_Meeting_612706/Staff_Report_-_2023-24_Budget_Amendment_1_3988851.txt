BACKGROUND

During the course of the year, there are occasions and circumstances that require an entity to
update its budget. This is a common and routine best practice to ensure that proper spending
levels are maintained in accordance with what has transpired throughout the school year.
Oregon Local Budget Law allows budget changes after adoption under prescribed guidelines.

This proposed amendment adjusts resources (i.e. revenues) within Fund 100 (General Funds),
Fund 200 (Special Revenue Funds), and Fund 400 (Capital Projects Fund) to recognize
additional resources. The proposed amendment also adjusts requirements (i.e. expenses)
within Fund 100 (General Funds), Fund 200 (Special Revenue Funds), Fund 400 (Capital
Projects Fund), and Fund 600 (Internal Service Fund) to meet the projected spending
requirements.

Note there have been changes to the amendment following the preview district staff shared with
the Board on December 12, 2023. Fund 100 and Fund 200 proposed adjustments have
changed, and the proposed amendment now also includes Fund 400 and Fund 600. The
resolution, slide deck and other accompanying materials provide details on the changes.

100 - General Fund Adjustments
Resource (i.e. Revenue) Changes
   ● Increase resources in Beginning Fund Balance to recognize the updated figure based on
       2022-23 Unaudited Actuals; State Sources to recognize the increase in the State School
       Fund; and County and Intermediate sources to reflect increase in MESD transit.
   ● Decrease resources in Local Property Taxes to align with valuation; Local Option Levy to
       recognize updated valuation figures from the Assessor’s Office; and Local Sources to
       align to prior year actuals and to reflect estimated increases in investment income.

Requirement (i.e. Expense) Changes
   ● Increase requirements in Instruction to reflect contractual agreement with PAT and
       temporary rate increase for substitute educators December 18-22.
   ● Increase requirements in Support Services to reflect contractual agreement with PAT
       and wage proposals for other units. The amendment also includes adjustments to
       account for costs related to Transportation and Maintenance costs.

200 - Special Revenue Fund Adjustments
Resource (i.e. Revenue) Changes
   ● Increase resources in State Sources to recognize the increase in Student Investment
       Account and Early Literacy Grant, net of the reduction in High School Success (M98).

Requirement (i.e. Expense) Changes
   ● Increase requirements in Instruction to reflect contractual agreement with PAT.
   ● Increase requirements in Support Services to reflect contractual agreement with PAT.
   ● Increase requirements in Enterprise and Community Services to reflect contractual
       agreement with PAT.
   ● Increase requirements in Contingency to reflect Student Investment Account remaining
       funds to be allocated.

400 - Capital Projects Fund Adjustments
   ● Increase resources in Beginning Fund Balance and State Sources to recognize the
       updated figures based on 2022-23 unaudited actuals.
   ● Decrease resources in Other Revenue from Local Sources and Bond Proceeds &
       Premiums.
   ● Increase requirements in Facilities Acquisition and Construction to reflect actual funds
       available.

600 - Internal Service Fund Adjustments
   ● Decrease requirements in Contingency and increase requirements in Support Services
        by the same amount to true up to actual spend.

RELATED POLICIES/BEST PRACTICES

Oregon Local Budget Law, Oregon Revised Statutes (ORS) 294.305 to ORS 294.565, allows
budget changes after adoption under prescribed guidelines.

ANALYSIS OF SITUATION

Should the Board choose not to pass this resolution, appropriation levels may be exceeded and
potentially in violation of Oregon Local Budget Law.

FISCAL IMPACT

No further impacts beyond that which have been outlined above.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT (IF APPLICABLE)

District staff members engage with the Community Budget Review Committee regarding
budget-related matters throughout the year.

BOARD OPTIONS WITH ANALYSIS

The Board may choose to pass or not pass the proposed budget amendment resolution. If
passed, the District will move forward with its amended spending plan for the Fiscal Year
2023-24. The consequences of non-passage would mean appropriations would not be
available for anticipated expenditures, and reductions would need to be made in order to stay
within appropriations and remain in compliance with Oregon Local Budget Law.


STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Board pass the proposed resolution to amend the 2023-24 budget.
