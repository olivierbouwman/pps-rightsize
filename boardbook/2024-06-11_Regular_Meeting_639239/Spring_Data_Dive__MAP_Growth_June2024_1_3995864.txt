Ongoing PPS Actions
 ●   We have provided Student Learning Analysis protocols for building leaders
     and instructional coaches focused on understanding the MAP data.

 ●   Professional Learning for building leaders and instructional coaches around
     Math and ELA instruction has and will be provided.

 ●   Senior Directors of Schools review their cohort’s MAP growth results and
     work with building leaders to make adjustments to focus strategies and
     efforts found in the School Continuous Improvement Plans (SCIPs).

 ●   Senior Directors have met with each school’s Instructional Leadership Team
     reviewing MAP data and deciding what and/or where to adjust.

Conclusions/Takeaways
1. Students’ mathematics growth decreased to below 50% for the
   current year, while reading growth remained steady by comparison.


2. It is likely that school interruptions have contributed to the current
   student growth results.


3. PPS has an ongoing theory of action to address reading and
   mathematics achievement across the district.
