                           PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
Shifts to Standards-Based Equitable Grading
Shifts to Key Practices on the Equitable Grading Practices Continuum
●   Mathematically sound scales
●   Performance standards
●   Rubrics
●   Mitigate Non-academic factors
●   Eliminate Bias-resistant practices
●   Provide multiple opportunities to demonstrate proficiency
●   Normalize feedback and include opportunities for student self-assessment
●   Use a standards-based or competency-based grading model
●   Calculate grades in ways that reflect a student's final proficiency

Next Steps
●   Draft of 3 year rollout is in final preparations and will build on the work of
    neighboring districts such as Beaverton SD.
     ○ This work would create standards based learning targets this year with
        teacher teams using work previously done in neighboring districts
     ○ Assemble teacher teams to modify work done previously to identify
        Learning Targets based on Oregon State Standards.
     ○ Pilot the work with some teachers in 24-25 school year for Math and ELA
     ○ Full rollout in 25-26 in 6-12 for ELA and Math.
