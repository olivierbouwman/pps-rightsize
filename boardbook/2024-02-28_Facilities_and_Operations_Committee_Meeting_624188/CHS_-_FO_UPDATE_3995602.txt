• Two-Site Option with Skybridge:

   o City code discourages skybridges, approval by City Council Required.

   o Additional costs of $25M-$35M for skybridge, parking lot, and construction
     logistics does not go toward education program spaces.

• Options with Partial Existing Historic Building:

   o Smallest amount of open space for the campus.

   o Higher cost and complexity of construction.

   o Limits flexibility of site design, building orientation, and circulation.
