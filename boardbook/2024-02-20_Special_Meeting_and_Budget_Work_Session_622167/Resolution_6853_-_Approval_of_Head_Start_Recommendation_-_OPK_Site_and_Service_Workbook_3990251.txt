                                       RESOLUTION No. 6853

                        Approval of Head Start Policy Council Recommendation

                                              RECITALS

A.   Federal requirements call for the Governing Board of a Head Start program to approve
     recommendations for the program.

B.   The Board of Directors for Portland Public Schools serves as the Governing Board for the PPS
     Head Start Program.

C.   PPS Head Start is required to submit a Site and Service Workbook annually as part of its
     Department of Early Learning and Care (DELC), Oregon Prenatal – Kindergarten (OPK) state
     grant. This data assists the DELC with report submission and facilitates any grant management
     systems claims PPS Head Start may have.

                                            RESOLUTION

The Board of Directors for Portland Public Schools, School District No. 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon,
approves the Head Start Policy Council recommendations as stated above.
