                                        RESOLUTION No. 6737

                                Appointment of Clerk and Deputy Clerks

                                               RECITALS

A.   To comply with the requirements of Oregon Revised Statutes (ORS), the Portland Public Schools
     Board of Education shall appoint a School District Clerk and Deputy Clerks for the 2023-24 fiscal
     year.

B.   ORS 332.515 requires the appointment of the Chief Administrative Officer as the School District
     Clerk. Deputy Clerks may also be appointed.

                                              RESOLVED

1.   The Portland Public Schools Board of Education appoints Superintendent Guadalupe Guerrero as
     Clerk of Portland Public Schools for the 2023-24 fiscal year.

2.   The Portland Public Schools Board of Education appoints Chief of Staff Jonathan García, and
     Director of Purchasing & Contracting Emily Courtnage as Deputy Clerks of Portland Public Schools
     for the 2023-24 fiscal year.
