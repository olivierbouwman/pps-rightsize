PROJECT NOTES
Progress:
 - Design reviews are underway week of 5/22/23 for all Phase 1B schools for security camera and intrusion detection systems. Construction documents for Phase 1A and 1B are expected to be
complete first week of June and to then go out to bid for installation. Design for phases 2 and 3 schools will start while Phase 1A/B is out to bid.
 - Classroom door locks (Hardware Upgrades 5999) are being installed in conjunction with accessible door handles and managed by the 2020 ADA project manager (please see 2020 ADA project
status update for additional information). This work is in progress.


Accomplishments:
- Design reviews have been completed for all Phase 1A schools for security camera and intrusion detection systems.
- Classroom door hardware contract for installation is executed and work is underway.

Risks:
 - Camera installation work requires additional rack space within technology support spaces, but new technology installations that are part of 2020 Technology-funded scope do as well. This is
requiring close coordination, particularly when one scope of work is being installed prior to the other.
 - Design reviews with school administrators is taking longer to coordinate than expected (but is important for confirming camera siting needs).
