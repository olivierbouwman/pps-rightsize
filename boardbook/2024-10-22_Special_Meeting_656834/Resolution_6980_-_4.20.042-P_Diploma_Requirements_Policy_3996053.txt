                                        RESOLUTION No. 6980

                 Resolution to Adopt Revised Diploma Requirements Policy 4.20.042-P

                                               RECITALS

A. On August 28, 2024, the Board Policy Committee reviewed and considered proposed revisions of the
   Diploma Requirements Policy 4.20.042-P.

B. On September 24, 2024, the Board presented the first reading of the revised Diploma Requirements
   Policy.

C. Pursuant to District policy, the public comment was open for at least 21 days, and there was no public
   comment received during the comment period.



                                             RESOLUTION

The Board hereby adopts the revised Diploma Requirements Policy 4.20.042-P and instructs the
Superintendent to amend any relevant administrative directives to conform to this adopted policy.
