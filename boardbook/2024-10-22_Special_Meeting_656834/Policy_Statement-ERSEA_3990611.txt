                                        ERSEA—Eligibility Policy

 Performance Standard
 §1302.12 Determining, verifying, and documenting eligibility.

Policy
Eligibility for participation in the PPS Early Learners programs will be determined by a Family Service
staff member. Determination will be confirmed at the enrollment appointment with the family,
preferably in person however documents may be sent electronically (text or email).

Procedure
Eligibility Training
All Family Service staff, Management team, and Policy Council members will be trained on eligibility
policy and procedure on an annual basis. New Family Advocates will be trained within 90 days of their
start date. New School Board and Policy Council members will be trained within 180 days of the
beginning of the term of the new policy council. Training will include, at a minimum:
     ● Methods on how to collect complete and accurate eligibility information from families and third
        party sources;
     ● Methods to treat families with dignity and respect, including ways to deal with possible
        domestic violence, stigma, and privacy;
     ● Information describing action that will be taken against families, staff, or participants who
        attempt to provide or intentionally provide false information.

Eligibility Requirements
A child is eligible for enrollment if:
    ● The family’s income meets guidelines set out by grant funders (ie. under 100% of Federal
        Poverty Line, under 130% of Federal Poverty Line*, under 200% of Federal Poverty Line, and/or
        is a member of an outlined priority population); or,
    ● The family is eligible for public assistance; including TANF, SNAP or Supplemental Security
        Income
    ● The child is homeless, as defined in part 1305; or,
    ● The child is in foster/kinship care.
If the family does not meet the above criterion, a program may enroll a child if:
     ● They would benefit from services, and provided that these participants only make up to 10
         percent of a program’s total funded enrollment.
     ● Family Service staff determining eligibility MUST receive written permission from the Program
         Manager prior to enrollment of over-income families.
     ● If a family is deemed ineligible for the program, FSW will refer the family to community
         resources or other service providers in their area.

*A program may enroll families in this income range provided these participants only make up to 35
percent of the program’s total funded enrollment.

Verifying age
Program staff must verify a child’s age according to program policies and procedures. A program’s
policies and procedures cannot require families to provide documents that confirm a child’s age, if
doing so creates a barrier for the family to enroll the child.
Children must be 3 or 4 by September 1st of the program year. In approved situations, a child may be
enrolled after their 3rd birthday as per Head Start 1302.12(b)(2)(i). See ERSEA – Enrollment Policy.

Verifying address
Program staff must verify a child’s address according to PPS district policy. Families will provide two
forms of mail, including at least one utility bill, to ensure the address is within school district
boundaries. *See PPS district policy related to residency and neighborhood school assignment. While
Early Learners is allowed some flexibility with residency this policy will impact students transitioning to
Kindergarten from the program.

Verifying eligibility
    ● Determine who is in the Family: all persons living in the same household and supported by the
      child's guardian’s income and are related to the child’s guardian by blood, marriage, or
      adoption; or are the child’s authorized caregiver or legally responsible person.
    ● To verify eligibility based on income, program staff may use tax forms, pay stubs, or other proof
      of income to determine the family income for the previous tax year or preceding 12 month time
      period.
          o If pay stubs are used to determine eligibility enrollment staff must make attempts to
               obtain documentation from the prior 12 month period. If this is not possible staff will
               clearly document the process they used to determine income based on documents
               provided.
          o Other proof of income may include tax documentation from the previous tax year
               (1040-Line 9, Total Income), W2s for all applicable adults in the household.
          o If the family cannot provide tax forms, pay stubs, or other proof of income for the
               relevant time period, program staff may accept written statements from employers,
               including individuals who are self-employed, for the relevant time period.
          o If the family reports no income for the relevant time period, a program may accept the
               family’s signed declaration to that effect, if program staff describes efforts made to
               verify the family’s income, and explains how the family’s total income was calculated or
               seeks information from third parties about the family’s eligibility.
    ● For Head Start funded slots a program may make an adjustment to a family’s gross income
      calculation for the purposes of determining eligibility to account for excessive housing costs.
      Program may use available bills, bank statements, and other relevant documentation provided
      by the family to calculate total annual housing costs with appropriate multipliers to:
          o Determine if the family spends more than 30% of their total gross income on housing
               costs and,
          o If applicable, reduce the total gross income by the amount spent on housing costs that
               exceed more than 30%.
    ● To verify whether a family is eligible for public assistance (Supplemental Security Income
      SSI/TANF/SNAP), the program must have documentation from either the state, local, or tribal
      public assistance agency that shows the family either receives public assistance or that shows

Eligibility Records
Each eligibility determination record must include:
   1. Proof of Address (two forms of mail)
   2. Copies of any income documents or statements, including declarations, that are deemed
        necessary to verify eligibility (e.g. pay stubs, tax returns, Income Statements, etc) or
        documentation of other eligibility categories listed above.
   3. Income Calculation Form (as applicable), which includes:
            a. Describing efforts made to verify eligibility, as required under paragraphs, collecting
                 documents required for third party verification that includes the family’s written consent
                 to contact each third party, the third parties’ names, titles, and affiliations, and
                 information from third parties regarding the family’s eligibility and/or;
            b. the process for calculating past 12 months income and;
            c. Number in the family
   4. Eligibility Verification Statement that identifies:
            a. The type of eligibility interview conducted
            b. How the family was determined eligible (which eligibility category)
            c. What documentation was used to confirm eligibility
   ● A program must keep eligibility determination records for those currently enrolled, as long as
        they are enrolled, and, for one year after they have either stopped receiving services; or are no
        longer enrolled.
   ● The Program Manager will monitor Income Verification and all eligibility documentation for
        accuracy and completeness throughout the year. PM will randomly select 10% of child eligibility
        records to monitor in depth in October and February each year.
