                                            ARTICLE III

           POLICY COUNCIL MEMBERSHIP DEFINITION COMPOSITION & SELECTION

A. DEFINITIONS OF REPRESENTATIVES
    In accordance with the Head Start Act, the Policy Council shall be comprised of two
    types of Representatives:
    1. Parents of children currently enrolled in the Early Learners Program, inclusive of the
       PPS Head Start Program. According to the Act, these members shall constitute a
       majority of the members on the Policy Council. PPS defines the parent as the child’s
       mother/stepmother, father/stepfather, or other family member who is a primary
       caregiver, foster parent, guardian, or the person with whom the child has been placed
       for the purpose of adoption decree. The parent can only represent the school of their
       enrolled child.
    2. Community Representatives. Community representatives must be drawn from the


                                                       T
       local community: businesses; public or private community, civic, and professional
       organizations; and others who are familiar with resources and services for
       low-income children and families, including for example the parents of formerly
       enrolled children.

B. MEMBERSHIP

                               AF
     Composition of the Policy Council shall include only primary caregivers (hereafter
     referred to as “caregivers”) of enrolled children, duly elected Parent Representatives
     and elected representatives of the community. All caregivers in the program will be
     invited to participate in the Policy Council.




      DR
    1. The Policy Council elected membership shall be representative of the number of
       classrooms per site. No site shall elect more than twice the number of classrooms at
       that site.
    2. All Representatives of the Community must be approved by elected parent members
       of the program. In no case, however, should Representatives of the Community
       exceed 50% of the total Council. Parents of former enrolled children may serve as
       Representatives of the Community.
    3. No Policy Council member may assist a member of his/her immediate family
       applying for employment to the Early Childhood Education program staff.
    4. Advisory members of the Policy Council are the Program Director and the Policy
       Council liaison staff. They may attend all meetings—unless specifically stated to the
       contrary—subject to the rules and procedures of public meeting laws. Further,
       advisory members will monitor to ensure that at least 75% of the caregivers
       involved meet Head Start eligibility requirements. Advisory members will ensure
       equitable representation for all Policy Council Board positions.
    5. All caregivers may attend Policy Council meetings.

C. SELECTION OF MEMBERSHIP AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST
    1. No member of the Policy Council can be employed by PPS Early Learners. No
       members of the immediate family of an employee may serve on the Policy Council. In
       addition, the Head Start Act states that members of the Policy Council shall (a) not
       have a conflict of interest with the program, and (b) not receive compensation for
       serving on the Policy Council or for providing services to the program.
    2. The State Representative will be elected by the Policy Council, serving a one-year
       term, and serve as an Executive Policy Council member. After each Oregon Head
       Start Association Conference, the Chairperson and State Representative will report
       back to the Policy Council, written or verbally, giving training when appropriate.
       Topics will be divided or assigned between the Chairperson and State
       Representative. The State Representative may attend State and/or National
       meetings when funds allow.

F. CONFIDENTIALITY
    1. The PPS Early Learners Policy Council is an official group of the PPS School District.
       Actions taken by members representing the Policy Council are an extension of the
       school district and those actions must be taken in accordance with Policy Council
       By-laws and school district policies. All information given to Policy Council members
       are given for the purpose of performing the duties of the group as dictated in the
       Head Start Act. Information must remain confidential. The information given to
       members may not be openly duplicated, distributed and/or shared with other
       parties that have no direct link to or official business with the Policy Council. Any
       caregiver and/or Policy Council member charged with a violation of the By-laws or a
       breach in confidentiality shall be dealt with by the Program Administrators. The
       complaint will be forwarded to the Policy Council Executive Board. The Executive
       Board shall address issues of breach of confidentiality. If a member is found
       responsible for a breach of confidentiality, appropriate actions will be taken by the
       Program Administrators. Those actions can range from written reprimand to
       removal from the Policy Council. There may also be consequences from the PPS

                                                         T
       School District in cases where breach of confidentiality may have legal or fiscal
       ramifications. That decision will be made by the Superintendent.

G. CONFLICT OF INTEREST


                                 AF
    1. A conflict of interest occurs when a Policy Council Representative/Alternate acts
       independently or contrary to the intent of the Policy Council’s purpose, policies,
       plans and actions.
    2. No person that is employed by PPS Early Learners may serve on the Policy Council.
       This includes, but is not limited to, substitutes, part-time, hourly, and seasonal
       employees.



      DR
    3. No person can serve as a member of the Policy Council while any member of his/her
       immediate family is employed by PPS Early Learners.
           Once a conflict of interest is identified, the Program Administrators will determine
           the nature of the conflict and determine the best course of action to remove the
           person with the conflict from the group. The recommendation to remove will be
           made to the Executive Board and the Policy Council at the next meeting.

H. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR POLICY COUNCIL MEMBERS
    1. We believe that people are individuals who have different thought processes. It is the
       role of every person in Early Learners to treat staff, families, and children with
       respect and dignity. It is an expectation that all adults, in the program setting
       conduct themselves in the following manner:
           a. With courtesy
           b. With respect
           c. With patience
           d. By dressing appropriately

           e. By treating people the way you want to be treated
           f. By modeling how we want our children to treat others
           g. By acknowledging and celebrating the cultures and practices of other people and
              families
    2. Under no circumstances will the following behaviors be allowed:
           a. Physical or verbal punishment of children
           b. Threats to staff or parents/guardians
           c. Swearing or cursing
           d. Smoking
           e. Quarreling, verbal fighting, raising of voices with others in a meeting or at an
              event
           f. Doing things that are against school safety practices and policies


                                                          T
           g. Bringing drugs, alcohol, or weapons to schools or Head Start events
           h. Violation of the Confidentiality Policy
           i.   Violation of any program policy



                                  AF
    3. Once a violation of the Code of Conduct is identified, the Program Administrators
       will speak with the member—privately if possible—to stop the inappropriate
       behavior. The Administrators will remind the members of the Code of Conduct for
       Policy Council members. If the behavior continues, the Administrators will take
       progressive supervision actions and offer the member referrals for counseling
       services. If the member persists after repeated warnings, the Administrators will
       determine the best course of action for the group. If removal from the group is the



      DR
       result, the recommendation to remove will be made to the Executive Board and the
       Policy Council at the next meeting.
    4. All information received concerning Early Learners families is to be confidential, i.e.
       phone numbers, addresses, family problems or situations. None of this information
       will be discussed outside of the Policy Council. Violations will result in removal from
       the Policy Council.
    5. When childcare can be provided by a member of the family (live in spouse/
       significant other), no childcare expenses will be taken from the Program funds.
    6. When approached by another parent with a complaint, the Policy Council members
       should refer the parent to the Grievance Procedure (the first step of which is to refer
       to the involved staff member).

A. MEETINGS




      DR
    1. Regular Meetings
           The Policy Council shall meet at least monthly between the months of October and
           June and on the call of the Chairperson. These meetings shall not conflict with the
           parenting classes offered by the program. A majority of the members present at any
           meeting may adjourn the meeting. The date and time of regular meetings will be
           decided by a majority vote at the first fall meeting of each year. All regular meetings
           of the Policy Council shall be open to the public. Notice of regular meetings shall be
           in writing and shall state the date, hour, and location of the meeting. Notices shall be
           mailed to each member not less than one week before the date of next meeting. A
           copy of the agenda and previous meeting’s minutes shall be enclosed with the
           notice. Copies shall also be available to all parents through classroom teachers.
    2. Quorum
           The presence of six (6) members of the Policy Council, including a minimum of two
           (2) officers, shall be required in order to constitute a quorum necessary for the
           transaction of the business of the Policy Council. No decision of the Policy Council
           shall be valid unless there is a majority vote of the members constituting a quorum.

    3. Agenda
           The agenda for each meeting shall be prepared by the Executive Council. Individual
           members of the Policy Council are encouraged to submit agenda items to the
           Chairperson, or Vice-Chairperson or to present their proposal formally under the
           agenda item of “New Business”. An item may be placed on the agenda by contacting
           the Chairperson at least five (5) working days prior to the regular meeting date.
    4. Special Meetings
           Special meetings may be called by the Chairperson or by a majority vote of the
           Policy Council. All members shall be notified by telephone if necessary.
    5. Meeting Process
           The Policy Council will be guided by Robert’s Rules of Order. The program will

      DR
