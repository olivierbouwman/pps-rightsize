Hi, my name is Kendra Lwebuga. My son, Kato, and I attended the April meeting of the Policy Committee and we
shared public comments about the “Credits Earned Prior to Ninth Grade” policy.

I want to thank the Policy Committee, especially Committee Chair Brim-Edwards, for giving this policy a second
look.

Before we brought this to the Policy Committee, PPS administrators told us that students who enroll in ninth
grade cannot bring highschool credits earned prior to ninth grade unless those credits were earned in specific
subjects (math and world languages, like Spanish). This left us with the difficult decision of whether to enroll at
Grant and repeat coursework or to attend school somewhere else and perhaps enroll at Grant in tenth grade,
when all credits would be recognized and recorded.

Fortunately, in our case, the original decision was reversed and my twin sons now attend Grant high school and
they are placed at an appropriate level in math and English Language Arts. The algebra, geometry and ELA
credits that they earned prior to ninth grade have been recognized and recorded.

Today, I am glad to see revised language in the policy that recognizes students who choose to transfer into PPS
as ninth graders and are bringing with them transcripts with highschool credits.

Anecdotally, both of my sons find the academics at Grant to be quite easy, painfully so when it comes to the
freshman classes (physics, living in the US and writer’s workshop). However, they have enjoyed taking
advantage of the many other things that go on in high school: cross country running, clubs, the homecoming
dance. New friends have invited them for karaoke and “dungeons & dragons” game play. So, the social life of
Grant is great. Academically, it would have been beneficial to have “honors” options to bump the rigor up just a
notch.
