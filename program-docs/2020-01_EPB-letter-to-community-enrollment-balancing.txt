Dear PPS Communities,



On ​February 9th and 10th, 2020​, community members are invited to attend Community Feedback
Sessions on PPS Enrollment Policies under review. As Portland Public Schools focuses on orienting our
school system towards providing every student with an equitable, inspired, intellectually rigorous
educational experience, the Board of Education and the Superintendent will launch an enrollment and
program balancing process to optimize the use of facilities within the school district while supporting
our efforts to reimagine the school experience for students - particularly at the middle grades. ​During
this process beginning in the Spring, staff will develop recommendations for enrollment and program
balancing in Portland Public Schools based on identified goals.




Why is PPS facilitating an enrollment balancing process?
  1. To help support the eventual Redesign of PPS Middle School programs that includes
        reconfiguration of identified K-8 neighborhood schools to K-5 elementary schools and 6-8 new
        comprehensive middle schools while maintaining regional K-8 school options to support
        program pathway continuity (for example: IB PYP to MYP and dual immersion language
        pathways).
  2. Optimize Use of Facilities at PK-12 to address under-enrollment and over enrollment, and to
        begin phasing out portable classrooms when possible to create connected, safer schools.
  3. Minimize Program Co-Locations of facilities between co-located programs and neighborhood
        schools at K-5, K-8 and 6-8 schools.




Prior to the Process:
Prior to beginning this process in the Spring, PPS will review enrollment and student assignment
policies to infuse a lens of racial equity & social justice, and review the legacy “sibling preference” rule
after hearing reflections from the community. During the month of February​, ​focus groups, public
sessions, and scheduled Board of Education Policy Committee Meetings are opportunities for interested
stakeholders to provide feedback in the process.
