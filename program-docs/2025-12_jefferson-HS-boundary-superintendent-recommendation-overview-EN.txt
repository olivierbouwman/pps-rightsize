 Superintendent’s Recommendation to Sunset Dual Assignment and Establish Attendance Boundaries
 for Jefferson and Nearby High Schools
 December 2025

 Jefferson has a storied history of student excellence and community pride. But choice-based dual assignment has led to
 shrinking enrollment and loss of programs. With modernization underway, it is time for a new approach that will revitalize
 Jefferson and provide greater opportunities for students.

 After robust public feedback on several enrollment plans, Superintendent Armstrong recommends Scenario C as the best
 option to transition Jefferson, Grant, Roosevelt and McDaniel from dual assignment choice to standard attendance
 boundaries.
