# Middle School Innovation & Redesign

**Our Mission**

We are committed to redesigning all middle schools through shifts in systems and policies, redefining the student experience, and supporting schools with solving persistent problems of practice, to provide equitable opportunities and outcomes for students, particularly Black and Native students.

Please submit your questions or feedback through this [form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfbBLWMaTp8KIRv8HVe7FJy3-v71lWMd76vBjjdXF6eFvXt_Q/viewform).

## **What is Middle Grades Redesign?**

**[PPS 2023-26 Middle Grades Redesign Plan](https://sites.google.com/pps.net/middle-grades-redesign/brochure)** is a set of practices, initiatives, and approaches that guide concurrent pilots to be brought to scale across each middle school and K-8 school (for 6-8th grade students) by the end of Spring 2026. The plan consists of these five priority areas outlined below.

* [Racial Equity and Social Justice](https://www.pps.net/departments/pk-12-core-academics/core-academics-6-8/middle-school-innovation-redesign#fs-panel-119106)
* [Inclusive and Differentiated Teaching and Learning](https://www.pps.net/departments/pk-12-core-academics/core-academics-6-8/middle-school-innovation-redesign#fs-panel-119109)
* [Professional Excellence and Support](https://www.pps.net/departments/pk-12-core-academics/core-academics-6-8/middle-school-innovation-redesign#fs-panel-119112)
* [Embracing Change](https://www.pps.net/departments/pk-12-core-academics/core-academics-6-8/middle-school-innovation-redesign#fs-panel-119115)
* [Integrated and Adolescent Supports](https://www.pps.net/departments/pk-12-core-academics/core-academics-6-8/middle-school-innovation-redesign#fs-panel-119118)

* Culturally Specific Community Partnership and Engagement
* Restorative Justice Practices
* Leadership and Mentorship
* Student Advocacy/ Positive Cultural Identity Development

* Engaging Standards Aligned Instruction + Responsive Classrooms
* Student Voice and Choice, including Project-Based Learning & Digital Portfolio
* Equitable Grading and Assessment

* PLCs and Structured Planning and Collaboration
* Professional Learning and Support
* Interdisciplinary Teaming and 8th Grade Success

* Effective Transitions (5th-6th & 8th-9th)
* Commitment to Continuous Improvement
* Innovative Scheduling

* [Advisory](https://sites.google.com/d/1p9DN1tz9Ssqize_C-Rw9NWoNBQdD9mSE/p/103_EiuKAOpqLi-U7utLSBTFDsMT5DUWo/edit)
* Middle Grades Programs- Co and Extra Curriculars (8th Grade Capstone/ Field Trip)
* College and Career Readiness
* Social Emotional and Mental Health
