# Effects of School Displacement on Academic Achievement and Wellbeing of Ethnic Minorities

**Citation:** Damm, A. P., Mattana, E., & Nielsen, H. S. (2022). Effects of school displacement on academic achievement and wellbeing of ethnic minorities. *Labour Economics, 79*, 102266.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.labeco.2022.102266 (open access, CC BY)

**Type:** Quasi-experimental (difference-in-differences)

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Denmark; closure of two school districts with high shares of ethnic-minority pupils combined with a dispersal/assignment policy.

**Sample:** Ethnic-minority children needing language support; some residents of closed districts were unaffected because of assignment rules, providing comparisons.

**Method:** Difference-in-differences.

## Key findings

- Strong negative effects of displacement on academic achievement that persist through lower-secondary school.
- Displacement delayed high school enrollment.
- Displacement raised absenteeism and lowered wellbeing toward the end of lower-secondary school.
- Authors interpret this as disruption plus negative exposure effects: moving pupils out of their local social environment into distant schools with different peers and fewer resources lowered achievement and wellbeing.

## Limitations

- Specific context (dispersal of language-support pupils to distant schools).

## Relevance to PPS

Relevant to PPS English learners and dual-language programs: displacing language-minority students to distant schools away from their community and supports can cause lasting harm. Keep cohorts and supports together.
