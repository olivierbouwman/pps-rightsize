# The Effects of Closing Urban Schools on Students' Academic and Behavioral Outcomes: Evidence from Philadelphia

**Citation:** Steinberg, M. P., & MacDonald, J. M. (2019). The effects of closing urban schools on students' academic and behavioral outcomes: Evidence from Philadelphia. *Economics of Education Review, 69*, 25-60.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econedurev.2018.12.005

**Type:** Quasi-experimental

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** School District of Philadelphia, 2012-13 and 2013-14 closures (about 30 schools), driven by both declining enrollment and low performance.

**Sample:** Displaced students and students in receiving schools.

**Method:** Difference-in-differences style comparisons of displaced and receiving-school students with comparison students; dose-response by share of displaced students and travel distance.

## Key findings

- Closures had no effect on the average achievement of displaced students; achievement rose among displaced students who attended higher-performing schools.
- Achievement of students already in receiving schools was negatively affected by the arrival of displaced students.
- School absences rose significantly for displaced students after closure.
- Achievement of both displaced and receiving-school students fell as the share of displaced students in a receiving school rose (a crowding/integration effect).
- Displaced students who traveled farther to their new school missed more days and received more suspension days.

## Limitations

- Effect sizes not reproduced here (paywalled).
- Single district during a period of severe budget cuts that coincided with closures.

## Relevance to PPS

Among the most directly relevant studies: it was a joint enrollment- and budget-driven closure round, it measures receiving-school effects, attendance, discipline and travel distance, and it shows that concentrating many displaced students in one receiving school harms both groups. Implication for PPS: cap the share of newcomers per receiving school and minimize travel distance.
