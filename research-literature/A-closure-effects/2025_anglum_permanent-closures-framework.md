# Assessing Permanent School Closures: A Conceptual Framework

**Citation:** Anglum, J. C., Rohde-Collins, D., Jabbari, J., & Chun, Y. (2025). *Assessing permanent school closures: A conceptual framework* (EdWorkingPaper No. 25-1229). Annenberg Institute at Brown University.

**DOI / URL:** ERIC ED674102: https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED674102.pdf (Annenberg EdWorkingPaper 25-1229)

**Type:** Conceptual framework / literature review (working paper)

**Peer-reviewed:** N

**Setting:** United States.

**Sample:** Existing permanent-closure literature.

**Method:** Conceptual synthesis.

## Key findings

- Anticipates a new wave of closures driven by widespread enrollment decline, the end of ESSER funds and federal uncertainty.
- Argues the existing literature, built mostly on performance-driven urban closures, often fails to inform the range of contexts in which future closures will occur.
- Proposes a framework covering schooling and local context, closure process and dynamics, analytic design, and a broad set of outcomes (students, staff, families, neighborhoods, finances).

## Limitations

- Working paper, not peer-reviewed; no new causal estimates.

## Relevance to PPS

Directly addresses enrollment- and fiscal-driven closures like PPS; useful checklist for what PPS should measure before and after closures.
