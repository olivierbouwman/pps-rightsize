# The Effect of School Consolidation on Student Achievement

**Citation:** Thorsen, H. S. (2017). *The effect of school consolidation on student achievement* (Working paper; SSRN 3033803; NTNU Open handle 11250/2451927).

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.3033803

**Type:** Quasi-experimental (working paper)

**Peer-reviewed:** N

**Setting:** Norway; consolidation of 76 rural lower-secondary schools.

**Sample:** Students exposed to consolidation, using national register data.

**Method:** Comparison of exposed and unexposed students with register data.

## Key findings

- No indication that consolidation reduced educational achievement on exams.
- A negative association between closure and grade point average appears to be driven by lenient grading in small schools rather than real learning loss.

## Limitations

- Working paper; not peer-reviewed. Rural lower-secondary only.

## Relevance to PPS

Adds Nordic evidence that consolidation of small schools need not harm learning; also a caution that teacher-assigned grades can shift for reasons unrelated to learning.
