# Regional and Rural School Consolidation: A Scoping Study of Research Literature

**Citation:** Eacott, S., & Freeborn, A. (2020). Regional and rural school consolidation: A scoping study of research literature. *International Journal of Educational Management*.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1108/IJEM-08-2019-0318

**Type:** Scoping review

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** International (motivated by consolidation reforms in regional New South Wales, Australia).

**Sample:** 35 empirical papers on school consolidation in regional, rural and remote areas published since 2000.

**Method:** Scoping study coding year, country, unit of analysis, data, timeframe and theoretical model.

## Key findings

- There is a limited evidence base that consolidation improves student outcomes; evidence is at best mixed.
- Communities losing their local school experience a range of social effects, amplified in rural and remote locations.
- Calls for more rigorous evaluation before and after consolidation.

## Limitations

- Scoping review maps the literature but does not appraise study quality or pool effects.

## Relevance to PPS

Confirms the thin causal base for consolidation benefits; supports caution about expected gains from merging schools.
