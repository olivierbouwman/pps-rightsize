# Topic A: Effects of school closures and consolidations on students. Index

Research base for analyzing the Portland Public Schools rightsizing plan. Each row links to a one-page summary in this folder (research-literature/A-closure-effects/). Open-access PDFs and text extracts are saved next to the summary where available; paywalled papers are summarized from abstracts only.

Order: reviews and syntheses first; then peer-reviewed quasi-experimental studies (regression discontinuity and instrumental-variable designs first, then difference-in-differences and panel designs); then working papers; then peer-reviewed descriptive and qualitative work; then research-consortium reports, which are **not peer-reviewed**. No formal meta-analysis of school-closure effects was found.

"Peer-reviewed" means a refereed journal article. NEPC briefs are reviewed by NEPC's editorial board but are marked N here.
