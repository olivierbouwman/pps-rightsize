# Effects of School Closures on Displaced Students and Future Cohorts

**Citation:** Taghizadeh, J. L. (2020). Effects of school closures on displaced students and future cohorts. *Labour Economics, 67*, 101910.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.labeco.2020.101910 (open access, CC BY)

**Type:** Quasi-experimental (family fixed effects)

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Sweden; all public middle-school closures 2000-2012 in a universal voucher system.

**Sample:** Displaced students and later cohorts who would have attended the closed schools.

**Method:** Family (sibling) fixed effects using national registry data with detailed school-quality measures.

## Key findings

- Overall, closures had no effect on student achievement.
- Effects on later cohorts were similar to effects on displaced students.
- Sweden's extensive voucher system did not appear to direct displaced students to higher-quality schools.

## Limitations

- Middle schools only; choice-heavy system.

## Relevance to PPS

Large-scale, enrollment- and performance-mixed closures with null average effects; also shows that school choice alone does not ensure displaced students land in better schools. Relevant to PPS where families may opt out of designated receiving schools.
