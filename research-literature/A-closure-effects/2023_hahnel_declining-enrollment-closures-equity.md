# Declining Enrollment, School Closures, and Equity Considerations

**Citation:** Hahnel, C., & Pearman, F. A., II. (2023). *Declining enrollment, school closures, and equity considerations* (Policy brief). Policy Analysis for California Education (PACE), Stanford University. (Companion working paper: Pearman, Luong & Greene, 2023, *Examining racial (in)equity in school-closure patterns in California*.)

**DOI / URL:** https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED632974.pdf (ERIC ED632974); https://edpolicyinca.org

**Type:** Report-not-peer-reviewed (descriptive analysis plus policy guidance)

**Peer-reviewed:** N

**Setting:** California, statewide, in the context of post-pandemic enrollment decline.

**Sample:** California public schools.

**Method:** Descriptive and regression analysis of closure patterns, plus a synthesis of equity guidance.

## Key findings

- Closures are far more common in schools serving higher shares of Black students.
- Even after controlling for a range of factors, the odds of closure rise by nearly 25 percent for each 10-percentage-point increase in the share of Black students.
- Places these disparities within histories of segregation, neighborhood disinvestment and gentrification, and offers suggestions for centering equity in enrollment-driven closure processes.

## Limitations

- Not peer-reviewed; does not estimate student outcome effects.

## Relevance to PPS

Among the most directly analogous items: a West Coast state, enrollment decline after the pandemic, and budget shortfalls. Supports a PPS equity audit of which student groups are affected by the closure list.
