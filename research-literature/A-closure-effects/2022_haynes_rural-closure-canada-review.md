# The Impacts of School Closure on Rural Communities in Canada: A Review

**Citation:** Haynes, M. (2022). The impacts of school closure on rural communities in Canada: A review. *The Rural Educator, 43*(2), 60-74.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.55533/2643-9662.1321

**Type:** Literature review

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Canada (rural), with reference to international literature.

**Sample:** Literature on rural school closure and consolidation driven by low enrollment and fiscal constraint.

**Method:** Narrative literature review.

## Key findings

- Closures in rural Canada are driven mainly by low enrollment and fiscal constraint and are highly contested.
- Reported impacts fall into economic, social and student-centred categories; student-centred impacts include longer bus rides and loss of small-school relationships.
- There is a relative dearth of Canadian research on community-level impacts; much of the evidence is qualitative.

## Limitations

- Little quantitative causal evidence on Canadian student outcomes; review is descriptive.

## Relevance to PPS

One of the few Canadian syntheses, and explicitly about enrollment-driven closure; weak transferability to an urban district but useful on travel burden and community effects.
