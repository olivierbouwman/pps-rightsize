# Are Students in Receiving Schools Hurt by the Closing of Low-Performing Schools? Effects of School Closures on Receiving Schools in Sweden 2000-2016

**Citation:** Taghizadeh, J. L. (2020). Are students in receiving schools hurt by the closing of low-performing schools? Effects of school closures on receiving schools in Sweden 2000-2016. *Economics of Education Review, 78*, 102020.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econedurev.2020.102020

**Type:** Quasi-experimental (family fixed effects)

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Sweden; all middle schools that received displaced students, 2000-2016.

**Sample:** Students in receiving schools.

**Method:** Family fixed effects with registry data.

## Key findings

- The inflow of displaced students lowered average grades and test scores in receiving schools, and the effect grew over time.
- However, the achievement of non-displaced students already in receiving schools was not affected, even when displaced students came from relatively low-performing schools.
- So the drop in receiving-school averages reflects composition (who is enrolled), not harm to incumbent students.

## Limitations

- Middle schools only; magnitudes not reproduced (paywalled).

## Relevance to PPS

Important nuance for PPS receiving schools: school-level averages may fall after consolidation simply because of composition, which should not be read as harm to existing students. Contrasts with Philadelphia and Michigan findings of modest negative spillovers.
