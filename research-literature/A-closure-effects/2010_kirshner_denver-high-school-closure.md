# Tracing Transitions: The Effect of High School Closure on Displaced Students

**Citation:** Kirshner, B., Gaertner, M., & Pozzoboni, K. (2010). Tracing transitions: The effect of high school closure on displaced students. *Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 32*(3), 407-429.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373710376823

**Type:** Mixed methods (quantitative comparison plus qualitative case study)

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Denver, Colorado; closure of a low-performing urban high school.

**Sample:** Latino and African American high school students displaced the year after closure.

**Method:** Multiple methods: quantitative analyses of academic performance against comparison cohorts, plus interviews on students' interpretation of closure and transition experiences.

## Key findings

- The transition cohort's academic performance declined after transferring to new schools.
- Qualitative findings describe how students interpreted the closure and the difficulties of joining new schools.
- Closure added stressors for students already coping with challenges associated with urban poverty.

## Limitations

- Single school case study; small sample, limited causal identification.

## Relevance to PPS

Shows the student-experience side of displacement (stigma, loss of relationships), relevant to supporting displaced PPS students, particularly older students.
