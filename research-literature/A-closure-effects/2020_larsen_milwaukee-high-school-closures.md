# Does Closing Schools Close Doors? The Effect of High School Closings on Achievement and Attainment

**Citation:** Larsen, M. F. (2020). Does closing schools close doors? The effect of high school closings on achievement and attainment. *Economics of Education Review, 76*, 101980.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econedurev.2020.101980

**Type:** Quasi-experimental

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Milwaukee Public Schools, Wisconsin; high school closures.

**Sample:** Five freshman cohorts (2005-06 to 2009-10) followed through high school.

**Method:** Student-level panel comparing students in closing and non-closing high schools.

## Key findings

- On average, closings were a negative shock: lower GPA and lower attendance.
- Effects lessen over time, but for many students high school ends before they fully recover.
- Closures lowered the probability of high school graduation and of college attendance, suggesting lasting consequences.

## Limitations

- High-school-only; single district.
- Effect sizes not reproduced here (paywalled).

## Relevance to PPS

Evidence that disruption can affect long-run attainment, not just test scores. PPS closures are mostly elementary, where recovery time is longer, but the middle-school closure falls closer to this older-student risk.
