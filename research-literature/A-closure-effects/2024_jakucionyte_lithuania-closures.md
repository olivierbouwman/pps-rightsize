# School Closures and Their Implications for Student Outcomes: Evidence from Lithuania

**Citation:** Jakucionyte, E., Pusevaite, I., & Singh, S. (2024). School closures and their implications for student outcomes: Evidence from Lithuania. *Journal of Education and Work, 37*(1), 67-91.

**DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/13639080.2024.2335463

**Type:** Quasi-experimental (propensity score matching)

**Peer-reviewed:** Y

**Setting:** Lithuania, 2013-2017.

**Sample:** Students in the final years of high school; followed after graduation.

**Method:** Propensity score matching with administrative data.

## Key findings

- Closure had a small negative effect on only some exam outcomes and on the probability of enrolling at university.
- Authors conclude the disruption effect is small even for students in the final years of high school.

## Limitations

- Matching on observables only; upper-secondary focus.

## Relevance to PPS

Recent European evidence suggesting modest disruption effects. Weak transferability to elementary grades.
