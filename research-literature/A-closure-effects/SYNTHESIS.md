# Topic A synthesis: what closures and consolidations do to students

This summary covers 41 items listed in [INDEX.md](INDEX.md). The file names in brackets refer to the summaries in research-literature/A-closure-effects/.

## Overall picture

No formal meta-analysis of school-closure effects exists. The closest syntheses are narrative reviews ([2019_tieken_school-closure-spatial-injustice.md], [2022_ewing_closure-literature-trends.md], [2017_sunderman_closure-low-performance-review.md], [2011_howley_consolidation-research-review.md], [2020_eacott_rural-consolidation-scoping-review.md]).

Across the quasi-experimental studies, the average effect of closure on displaced students' test scores is **small and usually close to zero after a year or two**. Behind that average are two consistent patterns:

1. **There is a short-term disruption cost.** It often begins in the announcement year, before any move takes place. In Chicago, students lost about 1.5 months of reading in the year closures were announced ([2009_delatorre_when-schools-close-chicago.md], [2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]). Short-run declines also appear in Denmark ([2018_beuchert_denmark-consolidation-disruption.md]), Pittsburgh ([2012_engberg_pittsburgh-closures.md]), Denver ([2010_kirshner_denver-high-school-closure.md]) and Texas ([2023_kim_texas-closures-long-run.md]).
2. **Outcomes depend on the receiving school.** Displaced students who move to clearly higher-performing or higher value-added schools tend to recover or gain. Those who move to similar or weaker schools do not ([2012_engberg_pittsburgh-closures.md], [2014_brummet_michigan-closures.md], [2019_steinberg_philadelphia-closures.md], [2017_credo_lights-off.md], [2023_bross_new-orleans-closure-restart.md], [2016_stroub_houston-closures.md]). The student-mobility review in [2017_welsh_student-mobility-review.md] reaches the same conclusion.

## Where studies agree

- **Placement into better schools is limited in practice.** In Chicago 2001-06, only 6 percent of displaced students reached top-quartile schools ([2009_delatorre_when-schools-close-chicago.md]). Across 26 states, fewer than half landed in better schools ([2017_credo_lights-off.md]). In Houston, Black and Hispanic students and lower achievers were less likely to reach high-performing schools ([2016_stroub_houston-closures.md]). Even universal school choice in Sweden did not route displaced students to better schools ([2020_taghizadeh_sweden-displaced-future-cohorts.md]).
- **Closures fall disproportionately on low-income communities and communities of color**, even after accounting for enrollment and achievement ([2024_harris_national-closure-patterns.md], [2023_hahnel_declining-enrollment-closures-equity.md], [2017_credo_lights-off.md], [2019_tieken_school-closure-spatial-injustice.md], [2026_fagan_england-falling-rolls-closure.md]).
- **Consolidating small schools is roughly neutral for achievement in the medium run** in the Netherlands, Norway and Arkansas: effects are zero to slightly positive ([2016_dehaan_netherlands-consolidation.md], [2017_thorsen_norway-consolidation.md], [2023_mcgee_arkansas-district-consolidation.md], [2023_chin_north-carolina-district-consolidation.md]). The promised cost savings often do not appear ([2023_mcgee_arkansas-district-consolidation.md], [2011_howley_consolidation-research-review.md]).

## Where studies conflict

- **Receiving schools.** Pittsburgh found no harm to students already in receiving schools ([2012_engberg_pittsburgh-closures.md]). Michigan found modest negative spillovers ([2014_brummet_michigan-closures.md]). Philadelphia found clear negative effects that grew with the share of newcomers ([2019_steinberg_philadelphia-closures.md]). In Chicago, reading in welcoming schools dipped in the first year ([2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]). Sweden found that school averages fell only because of who was enrolled, with no harm to incumbent students ([2020_taghizadeh_sweden-receiving-schools.md]).
- **Persistence.** Some studies find the losses fade within one to three years ([2009_delatorre_when-schools-close-chicago.md], [2018_beuchert_denmark-consolidation-disruption.md]). Others find persistent math gaps ([2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]), lasting harm for language-minority pupils ([2022_damm_denmark-displacement-ethnic-minorities.md]), or effects on graduation, dropout and earnings ([2020_larsen_milwaukee-high-school-closures.md], [2018_grau_chile-closure-dropout.md], [2023_kim_texas-closures-long-run.md]).
- **Attendance and discipline.** Philadelphia and NYC found higher absence among displaced students ([2019_steinberg_philadelphia-closures.md], [2020_bifulco_nyc-accountability-closure.md]). Chicago 2013 found no change in absences or suspensions ([2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]). Texas found lasting behavioral effects ([2023_kim_texas-closures-long-run.md]).

## Conditions that predict better or worse outcomes

- **Better:** a large, real step up in school quality ([2023_bross_new-orleans-closure-restart.md], [2017_credo_lights-off.md]); younger students ([2023_bross_new-orleans-closure-restart.md]); short travel distances ([2019_steinberg_philadelphia-closures.md]); strong relationships and teacher support in the receiving school ([2009_delatorre_when-schools-close-chicago.md]); gradual phase-out rather than abrupt transfer ([2015_kemple_nyc-high-school-closures.md]).
- **Worse:** long or uncertain announcement periods ([2009_delatorre_when-schools-close-chicago.md], [2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]); concentrating many displaced students in one receiving school ([2019_steinberg_philadelphia-closures.md]); longer commutes, especially for groups sensitive to distance ([2019_steinberg_philadelphia-closures.md], [2021_hannum_china-rural-school-closure.md], [2022_damm_denmark-displacement-ethnic-minorities.md]); students who were lower-achieving before the closure ([2020_bifulco_nyc-accountability-closure.md]); students from small schools ([2018_beuchert_denmark-consolidation-disruption.md]); older students near graduation ([2020_larsen_milwaukee-high-school-closures.md], [2023_bross_new-orleans-closure-restart.md]); moving the receiving school into the closed building, which raised attrition in Chicago ([2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]); and short-lived transition support ([2018_gordon_school-closings-in-chicago.md]).
- **Boundary changes alone** (reassignment without closure) show little disruption, and sometimes small gains, when they are well designed ([2023_hill_wake-reassignment-disruption.md], [2021_domina_wake-reassignment.md]).

## Implications for interpreting the PPS plan (cautious)

PPS closures are driven mainly by enrollment and budget, not performance. Most US causal evidence comes from closures of low-performing schools, where the potential benefit of moving to a better school is largest. When adequate neighborhood schools close, students are less likely to move to clearly better schools. That means the main upside documented in the literature may be smaller, while the disruption cost and receiving-school effects remain. The most analogous cases are Chicago 2013, Philadelphia 2013, Pittsburgh, Denmark and the English falling-roll closures. Taken together, they suggest academic effects that are neutral at best and negative in some cases, along with real costs for receiving schools and communities.

Swedish evidence finds no measurable effect from changing grade configuration ([2019_holmlund_sweden-grade-configuration.md]). US evidence on K-8 versus middle schools belongs to another topic area.

## Main gaps

- **Few rigorous studies of purely enrollment-driven closures** of non-failing elementary schools in the US.
- **Students with disabilities:** almost no outcome evidence for students with IEPs. The one relevant study shows that utilization formulas can steer closures toward schools with large special-education populations ([2025_weber_special-education-closures-chicago.md]). de la Torre and Gwynne found no change in special-education referrals.
- **English learners:** little US evidence. The Danish evidence is a warning ([2022_damm_denmark-displacement-ethnic-minorities.md]).
- **Leaving the district:** limited measurement of how many families leave for charter or private schools or move away. Chicago reported about 6 percent, similar to the usual rate.
- **Class size and capacity:** receiving-school class size and crowding after consolidation are rarely measured directly.
- **Long-run and well-being outcomes:** few studies go beyond test scores, and most of those are working papers ([2023_kim_texas-closures-long-run.md]).
