# Closed Schools, Open Markets: A Hot Spot Spatial Analysis of School Closures and Charter Openings in Detroit

- **Citation:** Green, T. L., Sanchez, J. D., & Castro, A. J. (2019). Closed schools, open markets: A hot spot spatial analysis of school closures and charter openings in Detroit. *AERA Open, 5*(2).
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419850097
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (GIS, descriptive)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 3. What happens to closed buildings (reuse, vacancy, charters, portfolio models)

## Setting
Detroit, closures of traditional public schools and charter openings across three policy eras.

## Method
Hot-spot (Getis-Ord) spatial analysis.

## Key findings
- Charter openings were more spatially concentrated (in 'hot spots') than traditional public school closures.
- Interprets the pattern through 'rollback and rollout neoliberalism': the public system retreats while a market system expands.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Descriptive; critical framework. Detroit's scale of population loss is extreme.

**Relevance to PPS:** Shows how closures and charter growth interact spatially. Oregon's charter sector is small, but PPS buildings sold or leased to charters could still draw enrollment.
