# PPS approves plan to close and consolidate schools, reversing board's November rejection

- **Citation:** Mistry, L. (2026, May 27). PPS approves plan to close and consolidate schools, reversing board's November rejection. *PublicSource.*
- **DOI / URL:** https://www.publicsource.org/pps-school-closure-vote/
- **Type:** News (last-resort source)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
Pittsburgh Public Schools, after decades of closures (93 buildings in 1997 to 54 by 2013, per RFA 2013).

## Method
News report.

## Key findings
- October 2024: a recommendation to close 14 schools was paused a month later for 'more thoughtful planning' (The 74, 2025).
- November 2025: the board rejected the plan. May 2026: it approved a revised plan 6-2 (1 abstention) to close nine buildings and 12 schools, with major changes starting in 2027-28 and a one-year transition period added by amendment.
- A swing vote cited new detail from the administration on 'learning opportunities, supporting data, identifying costs and implementation steps'. New board members also changed the balance.

## Limitations / viewpoint
News.

**Relevance to PPS:** Shows that a detailed plan, with cost estimates and a transition year, won approval after a first plan failed. It points to what PPS should publish before December 2026.
