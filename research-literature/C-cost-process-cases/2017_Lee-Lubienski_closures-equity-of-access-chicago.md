# The Impact of School Closures on Equity of Access in Chicago

- **Citation:** Lee, J., & Lubienski, C. (2017). The impact of school closures on equity of access in Chicago. *Education and Urban Society, 49*(1), 53-80.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1177/0013124516630601
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (GIS)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
Chicago's 2013 closures.

## Method
Spatial accessibility (student-to-school proximity) before and after closures, overlaid with density of children by race, child poverty and crime.

## Key findings
- Accessibility declined unevenly. The authors conclude that 'school closing may cause sociogeographic inequality in access to education', with losses concentrated where Black and low-income children and higher crime are concentrated.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Proximity-based access only. It does not measure school quality or travel safety directly.

**Relevance to PPS:** A method template: PPS scenarios can be checked for changes in walk or transit time to the nearest school, by neighborhood demographics.
