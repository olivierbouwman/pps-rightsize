# Predicting School Closures in an Era of Austerity: The Case of Chicago

- **Citation:** Weber, R., Farmer, S., & Donoghue, M. (2020). Predicting school closures in an era of austerity: The case of Chicago. *Urban Affairs Review, 56*(2), 415-450.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1177/1078087418802359
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
Chicago closure and turnaround decisions, 2003-2013, under Mayors Daley and Emanuel.

## Method
Multinomial logit models of building, student, geographic, political and neighborhood factors.

## Key findings
- The official rationales held: low test scores predicted closures and turnarounds under Daley, and underutilization predicted closures under Emanuel.
- But student race and proximity to the Central Business District and to charter schools also predicted closures, which points to factors beyond the stated criteria.

## Limitations / viewpoint
One district. Unobserved building conditions may confound the results.

**Relevance to PPS:** Even with published utilization criteria, outcomes can track race and real-estate geography. PPS could run a similar audit of its own scenario lists before the vote.
