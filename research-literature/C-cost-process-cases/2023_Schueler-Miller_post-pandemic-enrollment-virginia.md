# Post-Pandemic Onset Public School Enrollment and Mobility: Evidence From Virginia

- **Citation:** Schueler, B. E., & Miller, L. C. (2024). Post-pandemic onset public school enrollment and mobility: Evidence from Virginia. *Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis, 46*(4), 788-794.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.3102/01623737231178299
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 5. Declining enrollment nationally

## Setting
Statewide student-level data, Virginia, fall 2019 to fall 2020.

## Method
Descriptive analysis of entries, exits and transfers.

## Key findings
- Pre-K-12 enrollment fell 4%, with the largest drops in pre-K and kindergarten, driven mostly by fewer new enrollees.
- K-12 declines were largest among White and economically advantaged students (but in pre-K, among Black and disadvantaged students).

## Limitations / viewpoint
Early post-pandemic years only; one state.

**Relevance to PPS:** Losses concentrate in entry grades, which matches PPS's elementary-heavy decline. Kindergarten capture is therefore a key lever to test before assuming the decline is permanent.
