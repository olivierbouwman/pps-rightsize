# Mass School Closures and the Politics of Race, Value, and Disposability in Philadelphia

- **Citation:** McWilliams, J. A., & Kitzmiller, E. M. (2019). Mass school closures and the politics of race, value, and disposability in Philadelphia. *Teachers College Record, 121*(1), 1-44.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1177/016146811912100107
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (comparative ethnography)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
Rollout of about 30 Philadelphia closures in 2012-2013; two comprehensive high schools.

## Method
Multi-year ethnography, interviews, and coding of more than two dozen recorded community and district meetings.

## Key findings
- Argues the closure process itself (how schools were valued, how meetings ran, how communities were addressed) communicated the devaluation of Black communities, beyond any test-score or cost outcomes.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Interpretive, two schools, critical framework.

**Relevance to PPS:** Process matters in its own right. How PPS runs hearings and frames 'sustainability' affects trust regardless of the final list.
