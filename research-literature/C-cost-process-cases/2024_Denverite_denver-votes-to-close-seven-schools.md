# Denver Public Schools board votes to close 7 schools, forcing 1,100 families to relocate next year

- **Citation:** Zialcita, P. (2024, November 21). Denver Public Schools board votes to close 7 schools, forcing 1,100 families to relocate next year. *Denverite.*
- **DOI / URL:** https://denverite.com/2024/11/21/denver-public-schools-closures-confirmed/
- **Type:** News (last-resort source)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
Denver Public Schools, declining enrollment (DPS grew to about 93,000 students in 2017 before declining).

## Method
News report.

## Key findings
- The board voted 7-0 to close seven schools (Columbian, Castro, Schmitt, International Academy of Denver at Harrington, Palmer, West Middle, and Denver School of Innovation and Sustainable Design). Five of the seven were majority Hispanic. Three other schools lost grade levels without a board vote.
- About 1,100 families had to relocate.
- Follow-up (headlines only): a parent group sued DPS over the decision (Rocky Mountain PBS, Dec. 29, 2024). The board considered a three-year pause on closures (Chalkbeat, Mar. 5, 2025) and adopted a rule to wait at least four years between closure rounds (Denver Post, June 12, 2025).

## Limitations / viewpoint
News; follow-up items not read in full.

**Relevance to PPS:** A recent peer that completed closures. Its legal challenge and later rule requiring spacing between rounds point to pressure for predictability, a lesson for PPS's long-range planning.
