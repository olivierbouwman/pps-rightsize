# Assessing Permanent School Closures: A Conceptual Framework

- **Citation:** Anglum, J. C., Rohde-Collins, D., Jabbari, J., & Chun, Y. (2025). *Assessing permanent school closures: A conceptual framework* (EdWorkingPaper 25-1229). Annenberg Institute at Brown University.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.26300/46b0-g527
- **Type:** Working paper (not peer-reviewed)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 5. Declining enrollment nationally
- **Local full text (open access / publicly posted):** research-literature/C-cost-process-cases/pdf/2025_Anglum_EdWorkingPaper_permanent-closures-framework.pdf (and matching `.txt` from `pdftotext -layout`)

## Setting
U.S., after ESSER funding ended.

## Method
Conceptual framework with descriptive CCD enrollment statistics.

## Key findings
- In 2023-24, 64.8% of public schools enrolled fewer students than in 2019-20. The median school was down 4.9%, and a quarter of schools were down at least 13.6%.
- Argues that past closure research (often performance-based closures in big cities) generalizes poorly to today's enrollment-driven closures. Proposes evaluating context, process, mechanics and a wide range of outcomes.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Framework, not an evaluation.

**Relevance to PPS:** Supports treating PPS's enrollment-driven closures as different from the 2010s performance closures, and gives a checklist of outcomes PPS could commit to tracking.
