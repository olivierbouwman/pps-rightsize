# Closing schools in a shrinking district: Do student outcomes depend on which schools are closed?

- **Citation:** Engberg, J., Gill, B., Zamarro, G., & Zimmer, R. (2012). Closing schools in a shrinking district: Do student outcomes depend on which schools are closed? *Journal of Urban Economics, 71*(2), 189-203.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jue.2011.10.001
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (quasi-experimental)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
An unnamed mid-sized U.S. urban district with shrinking enrollment that closed many schools in one round (contemporaneous summaries, such as RFA 2013, describe it as 'unnamed').

## Method
Student-level panel with fixed effects comparing displaced and non-displaced students, with heterogeneity by the quality of the receiving school.

## Key findings
- Displaced students showed small negative effects on test scores and attendance on average.
- Negative effects were smaller, or reversed, when displaced students moved to higher value-added schools. Which schools close determines whether closure helps or harms students.
