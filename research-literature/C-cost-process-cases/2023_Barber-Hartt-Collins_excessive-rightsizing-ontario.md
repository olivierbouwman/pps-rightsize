# Excessive rightsizing? The interdependence of public school closures and population shrinkage

- **Citation:** Barber, R., Hartt, M. D., & Collins, P. (2024). Excessive rightsizing? The interdependence of public school closures and population shrinkage. *Canadian Geographies, 68*(1), 34-43.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1111/cag.12870
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
Ontario public school closures, 2011-2016.

## Method
Descriptive and associational analysis of closures by municipal population trajectory and community characteristics.

## Key findings
- Closures occurred disproportionately in shrinking and smaller municipalities.
- Closure prevalence was associated with low income, low ethnoracial diversity and low educational attainment.
- Raises the concern that rightsizing may feed the decline it responds to.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Associational; Canadian provincial funding context.

**Relevance to PPS:** Uses the same 'rightsizing' vocabulary as PPS, and asks whether closures can worsen the enrollment decline that motivates them.
