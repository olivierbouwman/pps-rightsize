# Left behind: Voters' reactions to local school and hospital closures

- **Citation:** Nyholt, N. (2024). Left behind: Voters' reactions to local school and hospital closures. *European Journal of Political Research, 63*(3), 884-905.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-6765.12622
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (difference-in-differences)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
315 school closures and 30 hospital closures in Denmark, 2005-2019.

## Method
Generalized difference-in-differences using precinct-level vote shares.

## Key findings
- Mayors lost about 1.6 percentage points of the vote in areas where they closed a school.
- Right-wing populist parties gained in both local and national elections after local school or hospital closures.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Danish municipal politics; mechanism inferred.

**Relevance to PPS:** Quantifies the political cost of closures for elected decision-makers. PPS board members are elected, and lost trust may affect future bond and levy votes (PPS relies on local levies).
