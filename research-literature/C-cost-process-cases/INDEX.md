# Research index: costs, process, equity and community, building reuse, and peer-district cases (Topic C)

Compiled 2026-10-07 for the analysis of Portland Public Schools' rightsizing plan (11-14 closures; board vote December 2026).
Types: peer-reviewed (Y) versus reports, briefs, commentary and news (N). News items are a last resort, used only for recent district events.
Open-access or publicly posted full texts are in `research-literature/C-cost-process-cases/pdf/`.

See `research-literature/C-cost-process-cases/SYNTHESIS.md` for the narrative synthesis.
