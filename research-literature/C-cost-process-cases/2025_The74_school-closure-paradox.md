# Exclusive Data Highlights Paradox: As Enrollment Falls, Fewer Schools Close

- **Citation:** Jacobson, L. (2025, May 12). Exclusive data highlights paradox: As enrollment falls, fewer schools close. *The 74.*
- **DOI / URL:** https://www.the74million.org/article/the-school-closure-paradox-as-enrollment-declines-fewer-buildings-are-shutting-their-doors/
- **Type:** News (last-resort source; reports unpublished Brookings data)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
U.S., 2014-2024; cases from San Francisco, Chicago, Pittsburgh and Seattle.

## Method
News analysis of Brookings closure-rate data.

## Key findings
- The national closure rate fell from 1.3% (2014-15) to 0.8% (2023-24) even as enrollment declined.
- Pauses and retreats: San Francisco halted closures weeks after announcing them (late 2024). Chicago's board barred closures until 2027 (WTTW, Sept. 26, 2024). Pittsburgh paused a 14-school plan. Seattle withdrew its plan.
- A California fiscal-oversight official (FCMAT) said boards 'avoid that decision as long as they can and at all costs'.

## Limitations / viewpoint
News.

**Relevance to PPS:** Retreats are common. PPS's December vote happens in a national climate where many peer districts paused, which raises the stakes of PPS's process design and evidence base.
