## Setting
California and U.S. schools, panel data 2000-2018. About 700 California closures in 2012-2021 displaced roughly 167,000 students.

## Method
Panel models of closure risk controlling for achievement, poverty, enrollment trends, charter status, urbanicity and district spending.

## Key findings
- Black students were 5% of California enrollment but nearly 14% of students in closed schools.
- Schools with more than 80% Black enrollment had about a 10% closure rate in California, more than twice the national rate for such schools. Schools under 20% Black closed less than 1% of the time.
- After controls, the odds of closure rose nearly 25% for each 10-percentage-point increase in Black student share.
- Conventional explanations (enrollment decline, poverty, achievement) did not fully account for the disparity.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Working paper. Includes charter closures, which are more common. Associational.

**Relevance to PPS:** West Coast evidence that closure risk tracks the share of Black students even after controls. PPS can run the same disparity check on its own scenario lists.
