# Regional and District Trends in Oregon's Post-Pandemic School Enrollment and Achievement

- **Citation:** Narayanan, M. (2025). Regional and district trends in Oregon's post-pandemic school enrollment and achievement. *Northwest Journal of Teacher Education, 20*(1).
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.15760/nwjte.2025.20.1.1
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (descriptive)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 5. Declining enrollment nationally

## Setting
Oregon districts, post-2020.

## Method
Descriptive analysis of state enrollment and assessment data.

## Key findings
- Large urban and suburban districts had notable enrollment declines, driven by a decrease in White students. Suburban districts gained Asian, Black and Multiracial students.
- Some large urban districts, PPS among them, mitigated pandemic achievement declines better than some suburban districts.
- Virtual-school enrollment grew.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Descriptive; small regional journal.

**Relevance to PPS:** Oregon-specific context: PPS's decline is partly White-family exit and suburbanization of families of color. That affects who remains in PPS and who is affected by closures.
