# Who Gets Closed? Twenty Years of Access-Restricting School Closures in Arkansas

- **Citation:** Tieken, M. C., & Murray, C. (2026). Who gets closed? Twenty years of access-restricting school closures in Arkansas. *The Rural Educator, 47*(3), 83-98.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.55533/2643-9662.1621
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
Rural Arkansas schools, 2003-04 through 2022-23, mostly closed after consolidation forced by under-enrollment or by academic or fiscal sanction.

## Method
Discrete-time survival analysis.

## Key findings
- Schools with more Black students had a greater probability of 'access-restricting' closure, especially as part of forced consolidation.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Rural setting and state-mandated consolidation, unlike a voluntary urban rightsizing.

**Relevance to PPS:** Recent evidence that racial disproportionality in closures holds outside cities too. It reinforces the case for a formal disparity analysis.
