# Centering Equity in the School-Closure Process in California

- **Citation:** Hahnel, C., & Marchitello, M. (2023). *Centering equity in the school-closure process in California.* Policy Analysis for California Education.
- **DOI / URL:** https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED633115.pdf
- **Type:** Research report (literature review + practitioner interviews; not peer-reviewed)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community
- **Local full text (open access / publicly posted):** research-literature/C-cost-process-cases/pdf/2023_Hahnel-Marchitello_PACE_centering-equity-closures.pdf (and matching `.txt` from `pdftotext -layout`)

## Setting
California districts with declining enrollment, plus closure cases nationally (Oakland, Pasadena, Denver, Jeffco, Philadelphia, New Orleans).

## Method
Literature review, enrollment and closure data, and interviews with practitioners and parent organizers.

## Key findings
- Savings: 'districts often overestimate the cost savings of closing a school'. Oakland projected $4-15M (about 2% of revenue) from closing or merging schools, and Jeffco (CO) projected up to $12M from 16 closures (about 1.4% of general-fund revenue).
- Why savings lag: teachers are reassigned rather than cut, there are one-time moving and renovation costs, transportation rises, and surplus property must be carried. Philadelphia pledged more than $60M from building sales after 2013 but generated about $24M. Later sales of $67.6M netted about $42M.
- Process recommendations: start early and transparently, set clear values-based criteria, share demographic and outcome data for closing and receiving schools, reserve seats in high-quality schools for displaced students (New Orleans priority placement; Oakland 'Opportunity Tickets'), and repurpose buildings for community use.
- California law since 2022 (AB 1912) requires districts in fiscal distress to complete an 'equity impact analysis' before a closure or consolidation.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Advocacy-adjacent policy report. District savings figures are projections taken from news reports.

**Relevance to PPS:** Practical design guidance close to PPS's situation: a West Coast district, enrollment-driven, under budget pressure. Its equity-impact-analysis model is a concrete step PPS could adopt before December.
