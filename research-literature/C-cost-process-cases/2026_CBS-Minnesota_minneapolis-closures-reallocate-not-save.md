# Proposals to close Minneapolis schools aimed at reallocating funds, not saving money, leaders say

- **Citation:** CBS News Minnesota. (2026, September 25). Proposals to close Minneapolis schools aimed at reallocating funds, not saving money, leaders say.
- **DOI / URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/minneapolis-public-schools-proposed-closures-funding/
- **Type:** News (last-resort source)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
Minneapolis Public Schools, proposals (September 2026) to close up to 16 schools amid enrollment decline.

## Method
News report of district-released figures.

## Key findings
- The general fund is about $217.5M. After the closures plus added student-facing positions, costs would be $218.9-220.9M. After utility and transportation savings, net savings would be only $0.72-2.3M.
- Leaders said the goal was to reallocate money from overhead to counselors, behavior support, intervention, arts and course variety, not to cut costs.
- About 86-88% of students would see no change. The district estimated that about 200 affected students might leave MPS.
- A $39.7M gap for the current year had already been closed without closures.

## Limitations / viewpoint
News; district projections, not outcomes.

**Relevance to PPS:** A useful contrast with PPS's deficit-driven framing. If PPS reinvests savings in receiving schools, as Minneapolis proposes, net savings may be small. PPS should say explicitly how much goes to the deficit and how much is reinvested.
