# The political effects of rural school closures - Evidence from Sweden

- **Citation:** Isaksson, Z. (2023). The political effects of rural school closures: Evidence from Sweden. *Journal of Rural Studies, 100*, 103009.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrurstud.2023.103009
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community

## Setting
All rural school closures in Sweden, 2013-2020 (geocoded).

## Method
Comparison of electoral and trust outcomes near closures with unaffected areas.

## Key findings
- Closures decreased trust in municipal politicians and reduced support for incumbents, most strongly for left-leaning parties.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Rural Sweden.

**Relevance to PPS:** Together with Nyholt 2023, evidence that closures erode civic trust. That matters for PPS's future bond and levy measures.
