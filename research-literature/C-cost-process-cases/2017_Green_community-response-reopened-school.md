# 'We felt they took the heart out of the community': Examining a community-based response to urban school closure

- **Citation:** Green, T. L. (2017). 'We felt they took the heart out of the community': Examining a community-based response to urban school closure. *Education Policy Analysis Archives, 25*(21).
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.14507/epaa.25.2549
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed empirical (case study)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 2. Equity, race, place and community
- **Local full text (open access / publicly posted):** research-literature/C-cost-process-cases/pdf/2017_Green_community-response-closure.pdf (and matching `.txt` from `pdftotext -layout`)

## Setting
A Midwestern city where a community-university coalition reopened a high school its district had closed.

## Method
Case study using interviews and documents, with social-capital and network theory.

## Key findings
- Closure was experienced as removing 'the heart' of the community.
- Leaders used networks to negotiate a community-university 'social contract', took coordinated strategic actions, and formed a community-driven education task force that reopened the school.

## Limitations / viewpoint
Single case; selected because it succeeded.

**Relevance to PPS:** Shows that closures can be reversed and that organized community coalitions are an outcome to anticipate. Partnership-based alternatives can be built into PPS's scenarios.
