# New Oakland Unified school board rescinds planned closure of five schools

- **Citation:** EdSource. (2023, January 12). UPDATE: New Oakland Unified school board rescinds planned closure of five schools.
- **DOI / URL:** https://edsource.org/updates/oakland-unified-school-board-rescinds-previous-boards-decision-to-close-schools
- **Type:** News (last-resort source)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
Oakland Unified. In February 2022 the board voted to close, merge or reduce grades at 11 schools over two years, citing a 34% enrollment decline from 2000 to 2020.

## Method
News report.

## Key findings
- The 2022 decision prompted protests and a hunger strike by two educators. Opponents argued the 'little-if-any cost savings' would not outweigh the harm.
- Parker Elementary and Community Day School closed in 2022, and RISE merged with New Highland.
- After the November 2022 election shifted the board's majority, the new board voted 4-2 (with 1 abstention) in January 2023 to rescind the planned 2023 closures of five elementary schools and a K-8 grade cut.
- Projected savings: PACE (Hahnel & Marchitello 2023) reports OUSD projected $4-15M, about 2% of revenue.

## Limitations / viewpoint
News; fiscal effects of the reversal not covered.

**Relevance to PPS:** Shows that closures decided over strong opposition can be reversed by the next elected board, wasting transition costs and goodwill. PPS's board faces elections too, so durable plans need broad buy-in.
