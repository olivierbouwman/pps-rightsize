# Seattle Public Schools drops contentious closure plan following months of waffling and backlash

- **Citation:** West, S. (2024, November 25). Seattle Public Schools drops contentious closure plan following months of waffling and backlash. *KUOW.* See also Jacobson, L. (2025, May 12). Exclusive data highlights paradox: As enrollment falls, fewer schools close. *The 74.*
- **DOI / URL:** https://www.kuow.org/education/2024-11-25/seattle-school-closure-plan-is-dead-for-now
- **Type:** News (last-resort source)
- **Peer-reviewed:** No
- **Subtopic:** 4. District case studies, 2008-2026

## Setting
Seattle Public Schools, facing a deficit of about $100M.

## Method
News report.

## Key findings
- September 2024: two scenarios to close up to 21 (or 17) elementary and K-8 schools, saving up to about $31.5M (or about $26M) a year.
- October 2024: the list was cut to four schools, with savings of about $5M (The 74 reports $5.5M), 'a fraction' of the deficit.
- November 25, 2024: the superintendent withdrew the plan, and the board unanimously approved the withdrawal and canceled the required hearings. Families described a year of stress and uncertainty.
- Later: in February 2026 SPS said it was 'not considering' closures for 2026-27 despite an $87M gap (KOMO headline), while its new superintendent said in May 2026 that closures remained possible (MyNorthwest headline).

## Limitations / viewpoint
News.

**Relevance to PPS:** The closest regional peer. Savings shrank as the list was negotiated down, and a long public process without a clear endpoint carried its own costs. PPS should show savings for the final realistic list, not the maximal one.
