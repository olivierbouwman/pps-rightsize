# Education outside the classroom increases children's reading performance: Results from a one-year quasi-experimental study

- **Citation:** Otte, C. R., Bolling, M., Stevenson, M. P., Ejbye-Ernst, N., Nielsen, G., & Bentsen, P. (2019). Education outside the classroom increases children's reading performance: Results from a one-year quasi-experimental study. *International Journal of Educational Research, 94, 42-51*.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijer.2019.01.009
- **Type:** Quasi-experimental
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 4. Outdoor, environmental, place-based and nature-based education

**Headline:** Denmark (TEACHOUT): one school year of regular (more than 2 hours/week) education outside the classroom had a small positive effect on grade 3-6 reading scores.

## Setting and sample
Danish primary schools; 20 intervention classes practicing regular education outside the classroom (udeskole) versus 10 comparison parallel classes, grades 3-6.

## Method
Pre/post quasi-experimental comparison of standardized reading tests.

## Key findings
- Regular education outside the classroom had a small positive impact on reading test scores over one school year.

## Limitations
Non-random assignment (teachers who chose outdoor education); few classes; one year; effect size not reported in the abstract.

## Relevance to PPS
This is one of the few controlled studies of regular, curriculum-embedded outdoor teaching (closest to Sunnyside's model) on a standardized academic outcome. The effect is small and positive.
