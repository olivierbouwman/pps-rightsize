# Achievement loss associated with the transition to middle school and high school

- **Citation:** Alspaugh, J. W. (1998). Achievement loss associated with the transition to middle school and high school. *The Journal of Educational Research, 92(1), 20-25*.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/00220679809597572
- **Type:** Descriptive (ex post facto, district-level)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 1. Grade configuration (K-8 vs middle school) and school transitions

**Headline:** Districts with a grade-6 middle-school transition showed achievement loss relative to K-8 districts; loss was larger when several elementary schools fed one middle school; middle-school districts had higher dropout rates.

## Setting and sample
Three groups of 16 school districts each (48 districts) with different grade configurations.

## Method
Ex post facto comparison of achievement trajectories across districts with K-8 versus 6-8 configurations.

## Key findings
- A statistically significant achievement loss at the grade-6 transition in middle-school districts compared with K-8 districts.
- The loss was larger when students from multiple elementary schools were merged into a single middle school.
- Both groups lost ground at the 9th-grade transition, but the loss was larger for middle-school students.
- High-school dropout rates were higher in districts with 6-8 middle schools.

## Limitations
District-level, non-experimental, small number of districts; older data. Confounding between district characteristics and configuration is likely.

## Relevance to PPS
The finding that losses grow when several feeder schools merge into one middle school applies directly to PPS. Students from converted K-8s will join peers from multiple K-5 feeders in larger middle schools.
