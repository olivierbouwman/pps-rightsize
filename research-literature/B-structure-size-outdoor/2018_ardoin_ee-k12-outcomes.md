# Environmental education and K-12 student outcomes: A review and analysis of research

- **Citation:** Ardoin, N. M., Bowers, A. W., Roth, N. W., & Holthuis, N. (2018). Environmental education and K-12 student outcomes: A review and analysis of research. *Journal of Environmental Education, 49(1), 1-17*.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/00958964.2017.1366155
- **Type:** Systematic review (descriptive analysis)
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 4. Outdoor, environmental, place-based and nature-based education

**Headline:** 119 peer-reviewed articles (1994-2013) on K-12 environmental education reported 121 distinct outcomes, most positive; the authors call for bolder, more diverse research designs.

## Setting and sample
Peer-reviewed studies of K-12 environmental education programs, 1994-2013.

## Method
Systematic literature search and descriptive coding of outcomes.

## Key findings
- 119 articles; 121 unique outcomes identified, spanning environmental knowledge and attitudes, academic outcomes, and personal and social development.
- Most articles reported positive findings.
- The authors highlight the versatility of environmental education and the need for stronger, more varied research designs.

## Limitations
Vote-counting of reported outcomes; publication bias likely; quality of underlying designs not pooled. Summarized from the abstract (publisher blocked full-text download).

## Relevance to PPS
This shows environmental education is linked to many positive outcomes in the literature, but the review was not designed to estimate effect sizes on achievement.
