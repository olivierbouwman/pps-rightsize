# School green space and its impact on academic performance: A systematic literature review

- **Citation:** Browning, M. H. E. M., & Rigolon, A. (2019). School green space and its impact on academic performance: A systematic literature review. *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 16(3), 429*.
- **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16030429
- **Type:** Systematic review
- **Peer-reviewed:** Yes
- **Subtopic:** 4. Outdoor, environmental, place-based and nature-based education
- **Open-access full text (local):** `oa-fulltext/2019_browning_school-green-space-review.txt`

**Headline:** 13 studies with 122 findings: 64% non-significant, 28% positive, 8% negative; the literature on school greenness and achievement is small, mixed and mostly observational.

## Setting and sample
Peer-reviewed studies of green space around schools and academic outcomes.

## Method
Systematic review of 13 articles.

## Key findings
- Of 122 findings, 64% were non-significant, 8% significant and negative, and 28% significant and positive.
- Positive findings were limited to greenness, tree cover and green land cover within 2,000 m of schools.
- Grades and college-prep exams showed more positive associations than math or reading test scores.
- Moderation by SES, gender and urbanization was mixed.

## Limitations
Concerns passive green-space exposure, not outdoor instruction; school-level observational designs.

## Relevance to PPS
This is an important caution. Simply having green surroundings is not reliably linked to test scores, so any Sunnyside benefit would come from active, structured outdoor pedagogy rather than setting alone.
