Ask PPS for a clear, student-centered “right-sizing” plan—and ask Salem to fund schools so disruption is not treated as the only option. Your message goes to all seven board directors and the two state legislators who represent your address.
Now is the time to make your voice heard
We are nearing a point of no return with “rightsizing”. Now is the time to speak up, and loudly. Write your school board representative and express your concerns – and don’t stop there.
There’s a highly contested gubernatorial election happening on November 3, and Governor Kotek has yet to speak out on this major issue that impacts so many of her constituents.
Let the Governor know how you feel. Do the same with the Mayor’s office and your local City Councilors.
