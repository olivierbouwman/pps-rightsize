District staff lay out 'well‑resourced' school framework as rightsizing begins
Sep 2, 2026
District staff told the Board's Teaching, Learning & Enrollment committee that declining enrollment—more than 6,000 students since 2018–19 and another projected 3,400 by 2031–32—has stretched resources and that a 'well‑resourced school' framework (stable staffing, rich programming, comprehensive supports, safe facilities) will guide rightsizing scenarios.
The full story
District staff presented a rightsizing framework to the Portland School District Board's Teaching, Learning & Enrollment committee on Sept. 8, saying enrollment and budget declines have left programming and staffing uneven across the system.
