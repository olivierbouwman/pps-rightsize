PORTLAND, Ore. (KOIN) – Portland Public Schools has adopted a new budget for its upcoming school year, and layoffs are underway for hundreds of staff positions.
The PPS Board adopted the new budget Wednesday after facing the challenge of closing a $50 million budget gap. The new budget is set to take effect on July 1.
“Sometimes we need to display courage and do hard things. And this is one of those times that we need to do really hard things,” said Michelle DePass, the vice-chair of the PPS board.
The proposed budget originally included cuts to more than 300 staff positions with 180 in schools and 142 support roles. The approved budget eliminated 320 full-time positions, including 80 teachers.
Teachers were in attendance at last night’s five-hour board meeting, where they raised concerns about generative AI and urged the board to prioritize student-to-teacher connections.
