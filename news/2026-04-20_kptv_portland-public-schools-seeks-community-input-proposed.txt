Portland Public Schools seeks community input on proposed school closures
PORTLAND, Ore. (KPTV) - Portland Public Schools is asking for community input ahead of a proposed plan to consolidate and close several schools.
A virtual meeting is set for Monday at 6 p.m. Another meeting is planned for Wednesday at Ida B. Wells High School, with both in-person and virtual options.
The meetings come as teachers and other district employees are set to be furloughed for about a week’s worth of school days.
In early March, Superintendent Kimberlee Armstrong spoke about the possibility of school closures.
“I anticipate presenting the board with a list of schools for closure in November, for board action in December, and notification out to community by January of this upcoming year, so no school closures this coming academic year but we are anticipating that there will be some closures,” Armstrong said.
The district has not identified which schools it is considering closing yet. The meetings are for parents, students, and community members to share their perspectives ahead of any decisions.
