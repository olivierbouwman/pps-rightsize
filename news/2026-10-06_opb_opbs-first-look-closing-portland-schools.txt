Top story
Up to 14 schools closing in Portland Public Schools ‘rightsizing’ scenarios
Portland Public Schools announced proposed scenarios yesterday evening that could result in massive changes for Portland’s elementary, middle and K-8 schools, including building closures, program consolidations and changes to school boundaries.
One scenario proposes 14 school closures, another proposes 11 closures, and a third scenario proposes to do nothing.
The district says the plan would ensure a more consistent standard of service across its schools. The plan has already drawn pushback from some parents and teachers.
Most recommendations in the scenarios would go into effect in fall 2027. The board is expected to vote on a final plan this December. (Elizabeth Miller)

One more look
Oregon’s grape harvest is in full swing, but wine sales have been dropping
Paul Durant owns Red Ridge Farms in the Dundee Hills. The vineyard is known for its fine wines and pastoral visitor center with beehives, lavender fields and olive groves. Durant thinks the company is doing OK, considering the 2025 Oregon Vineyard and Winery Census.
“We’ve never seen anything quite like this,” Durant said.
Last year, Oregon’s wine grape production decreased 25%. Grapes are being left to rot on the vine and whole vineyards have been plowed under. In total, 110 vineyards and wineries closed in Oregon last year.
And while it hasn’t been as badly hit, Oregon’s beer industry is dealing with the same issues. (Kristian Foden-Vencil)
