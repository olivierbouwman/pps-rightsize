Board puts rightsizing, facilities and community engagement on the evaluation calendar
Sep 17, 2026
Trustees agreed rightsizing and facilities should be treated as annual focus areas in the superintendent evaluation, with process-evaluation measures (community outreach, language accessibility, financial stewardship) to document steps taken in advance of difficult decisions.
The full story
At the retreat trustees and consultants identified rightsizing (boundary changes and school repurposing) and facilities oversight as major annual areas of focus to be woven into the superintendent evaluation. Presenters advised trustees to require process-evaluation evidence from the superintendent — for example, how many languages a survey was offered in, the reach of outreach to priority communities, and how budget and bond accountability were considered — so the board could demonstrate a transparent decision path when contentious proposals come forward.
