PPS considers last-second measure to restore cut positions
PORTLAND, Ore. (KATU) — The board of Portland Public Schools is set to approve its 2026-2027 budget at its meeting on Tuesday night, ahead of the start of the fiscal year on July 1.
Board members have proposed multiple amendments, including one from Stephanie Engelsman that would restore $13.1 million "for staffing in schools in student facing positions."
The amendment does not include a funding source or how many positions would be saved if it is approved. Engelsman did not immediately respond to a request for comment before the meeting.

The proposed budget for 26-27 includes $50 million in cuts to the district's general fund. A PPS spokesperson said it has issued layoff notices to 87 educators as of Tuesday.
The Portland Association of Teachers confirmed that all 87 are members of the teachers' union.
