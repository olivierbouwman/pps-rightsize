PORTLAND, Ore. — The Portland Public Schools Board is set to meet Tuesday evening to discuss the district’s "rightsizing" plan, a long-term effort that could lead to the closure of as many as 20 schools.
The meeting comes as Oregon’s largest school district faces declining enrollment, rising costs and questions about how it uses its school buildings.
According to PPS, the plan could include school closures as well as changes to programs, school boundaries and how some buildings are used. The district says the broader effort is intended to strengthen stability, equity and access across PPS communities.
Superintendent Dr. Kimberlee Armstrong said last week that the district has not yet determined how many schools could close.
"That number could be as great as 20 or as few as 10," Armstrong said. "I think it really depends on the decisions we make and the input on the board as we make our way through September."
The potential closures could affect thousands of students and families across Portland.
The board’s meeting is scheduled for 6 p.m. Tuesday at Grant High School.
