By Barbara Quinn
Portland Public School Superintendent Dr. Kimberlee Armstrong joined North Portland students walking to their first day of school at Rosa Park Elementary School in the Portsmouth neighborhood just before 8 am, Aug. 26. Students, parents and school staff walked the two blocks from McCoy Park while catchy music blared from a speaker as they picked up participants along the way.
Once arrived at the school, Armstrong welcomed the students to their first day and visited classrooms afterward. Also present as students arrived were District 2 School Board representatives Michelle DePass and Rashelle Chase-Miller.
