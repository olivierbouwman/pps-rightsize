# PPS Rightsizing: Program Documentation Index

*Moved from `PROGRAM_OVERVIEW.md`. Files named below live in `program-docs/`.*

Collected 2026-10-07 from pps.net and other official Portland Public Schools (PPS) sources. These are mostly pps.net pages, Google Drive files linked from pps.net, and Wayback Machine copies of pps.net pages that have since been retired.

**Not included here:** BoardBook agendas and attachments (meetings.boardbook.org, org 915) and YouTube transcripts. Another process collects those into `boardbook/` and `transcripts/`. Where a pps.net page links to a BoardBook file, the relevant BoardBook folder is listed in the "Board actions" section below.

**File conventions:**
- Each HTML page is saved as raw `.html` plus a cleaned `.md` (main content only, converted to Markdown).
- Each PDF is saved as `.pdf` plus a `.txt` made with `pdftotext -layout`.
- The `YYYY-MM-DD_` prefix is the document's own date where known. Otherwise it is the publication month or year. Live web pages captured today use `2026-10-07_`.
- `program-docs/PPS_*` files are standing board policies or reference documents that have no single date.

## Program summary

**What rightsizing is.** "Rightsize. Right Support. Right for Students." is PPS's districtwide process for consolidating and closing schools and programs. It also covers boundary changes, program relocations and grade-level reconfigurations. The Board launched it on April 14, 2026 with **Resolution 7296** (`boardbook/2026-04-14_Regular_Meeting_739410/`), as "phase three of Growing Great Schools."

Three pressures drive it:
- **Falling enrollment.** K-12 enrollment was 42,622 in fall 2025. PSU's Population Research Center forecasts 36,763 by 2035-36 (`program-docs/2026_PSU-PRC-PPS-enrollment-forecast-2026-27-to-2035-36.txt`). Elementary enrollment has fallen 28% in ten years (`program-docs/2025-10_PPS-enrollment-10yr-history-summary-2025-26.txt`).
- **Budget gaps.** PPS closed a $56M gap for 2026-27 by cutting 336 FTE. The superintendent projects a gap of more than $65M for 2027-28 and wrote that "we will need to plan for consolidation and closure" (`program-docs/2026-04_PPS-2026-27-superintendent-proposed-budget-message.txt`; also `program-docs/2026-01-20_PPS-2026-27-budget-development-preliminary-recommendations.txt`).
- **Buildings.** PPS runs more buildings than its enrollment supports, and many are aging and seismically vulnerable (`program-docs/2021-12_PPS-long-range-facility-plan-vol1.txt`, `program-docs/PPS_schools-on-city-URM-list.txt`).

Resolution 7296 says the outcome "will include school and program closures and/or consolidations." It also says every school and program is in scope.

**Predecessor efforts.**
- **Enrollment & Program Balancing, 2020-23.** Started by Resolution 6059 (`program-docs/2020-02-25_EPB-board-resolution-6059-scope-of-work.txt`). Its Southeast phase (SEGC) opened Kellogg and Harrison Park middle schools, converted K-8s and moved programs (`program-docs/2022-04-22_SEGC-phase-2-final-report.txt`, `program-docs/2024-12_wayback_pps-enrollment-and-program-balancing-page.md`).
- **SEGC equity audit, June 2026.** An independent audit of the SEGC by Green & Hanson, presented August 4, 2026, sets out lessons for this process (`program-docs/2026-06-10_SEGC-equity-audit-evaluation-report.txt`, `...school-by-school-analysis.txt`).
- **Jefferson dual-assignment boundary process, 2025.** The Board approved the superintendent's Scenario C in January 2026. It ends Jefferson dual assignment and sets new Jefferson, Grant, McDaniel and Roosevelt boundaries starting fall 2027. PPS called it "the first step in a systemwide review" (`program-docs/2026-10-07_pps-jefferson-is-rising.md`, `program-docs/2025-12_jefferson-HS-boundary-superintendent-recommendation-overview-EN.txt`).
- **Middle-grades redesign / K-8 to middle school conversions.** These shape the rightsizing grade-level changes (`program-docs/2026-10-07_pps-middle-school-innovation-redesign.md`).

**Decision criteria.** The Board adopted four Guiding Principles on May 26, 2026 with **Resolution 7307** (`boardbook/2026-05-26_Special_Meeting_746944/`; summarized in `program-docs/2026-10-07_rightsizing-frequently-asked-questions.md`):
1. Well-resourced, sustainable schools.
2. Safe and healthy learning environments.
3. Educational options: neighborhood schools are "the foundation," and access to choice programs is balanced against "assuring well-resourced neighborhood schools."
4. Use of the PPS Equity Lens, focused on Native and Black students, multilingual learners, students in special education and students experiencing poverty.

Every scenario must include a standardized equity impact analysis.

"Well-resourced" is defined by **sustainability thresholds** (`program-docs/2026-09-08_TLE-committee-well-resourced-schools-presentation.txt`; `program-docs/2026-10-05_rightsizing-scenarios-A-B-overview.txt`):

| Grade band | Minimum enrollment |
| --- | --- |
| K-5 | 360 |
| K-8 or middle school | 500 |
| High school | 1,100 |

The FAQ says no single factor decides closures. Factors considered include enrollment, utilization, operating cost, building condition and earthquake risk. Board Policy 6.10.030-P requires a School Closure Report before the Board votes on any closure. It must cover financial impact, five-year enrollment, facility, community and cluster impacts, and an operational plan with a new boundary map (`program-docs/PPS_board-policy-6.10.030-P-school-initiation-and-closure.txt`).

**Schools in play.** PPS released two scenarios on October 5, 2026, with figures projected to 2031-32 (`program-docs/2026-10-05_rightsizing-scenarios-A-B-overview.txt` plus four regional PDFs).

**Scenario A closes 14 schools:**
- 13 elementary or K-8 schools: Beach, Buckman, Creston, Irvington, James John, Lewis, Maplewood, Marysville, Peninsula, Rose City Park, Sabin, Stephenson and Woodmere.
- Sellwood Middle School.
- It also changes 38 attendance boundaries, moves 13 programs and makes 8 grade-level changes.
- About 27% of K-8 students would change schools, and 80% of students would attend a school above the threshold.

**Scenario B closes 11 schools:**
- The same list minus Stephenson, Rose City Park and Lewis.
- It changes 34 boundaries and moves 12 programs.
- About 24% of K-8 students would change schools, and 73% would attend a school above the threshold.

**Status quo (no changes):** only 48% of students would attend a school above the threshold.

Both scenarios also:
- Close the MLC high school program (grades 9-12). MLC becomes a K-8 and Odyssey moves to the MLC site.
- Move language immersion programs, including Spanish to Cesar Chavez and Mandarin from Clark to Woodstock.
- Move grade 6-8 students out of several K-8s. Examples: Beverly Cleary to Beaumont, Laurelhurst to Mt. Tabor, Faubion to Ockley Green, Vernon to Harriet Tubman.

Closures by region:

| Region | Scenario A | Scenario B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jefferson/Roosevelt | 4 | 4 |
| Cleveland/Franklin | 6 | 5 |
| Grant/McDaniel | 2 | 1 |
| Wells/Lincoln | 2 | 1 |

**Timeline and milestones** (`program-docs/2026-10-07_rightsizing-timeline.md`; October 6 board deck in `boardbook/2026-10-06_Regular_Meeting_769955/`):

| Phase | When |
| --- | --- |
| Exploration | Fall 2025 to winter 2026 |
| Plan + Initiate | Winter 2025-26 (March 10 and March 31, 2026 board sessions) |
| Engage + Develop | Spring and summer 2026 |
| Study + Recommend | Fall 2026 (current phase) |
| Decide + Commit | November-December 2026 |
| Implement | Winter to fall 2027 |

Key events so far:
- April 2026: board engagement sessions.
- April 14: Resolution 7296 launches the process.
- May 26: Resolution 7307 adopts the Guiding Principles.
- September 1 and 15: "well-resourced schools," magnitude of change and KPI framework presented to the Board.
- October 5: scenarios released.
- October 6: scenarios presented to the Board.

**Key dates ahead:**
- Community Connections and Community Voice sessions run through late October (`program-docs/2026-10-07_rightsizing-calendar.md`). Feedback is collected through the scenario survey.
- October 13: TLE Committee on dual language immersion and middle-grades rightsizing.
- November 4: Board meeting with the feedback summary.
- November 9: Facilities (FIO) Committee.
- Mid-November: Superintendent's recommendation published.
- **November 17: Superintendent's recommendation presented to the Board.**
- **December 15: Board vote on rightsizing.**
- Changes take effect in fall 2027 (no later than 2027-28) and must be completed within three years.

**Board actions required.**
- **Already taken:** Resolution 7296 (process launched, April 14) and Resolution 7307 (Guiding Principles and Workplan, May 26).
- **Still to come:**
  - A Board vote on the superintendent's rightsizing recommendation (planned for December 15). Under 6.10.030-P, any closure needs a Board decision based on a superintendent recommendation plus School Closure Reports.
  - Board approval of boundary changes, following the process in 4.10.045-P and 4.10.049-AD.
  - Board approval of the implementation plan, which Resolution 7307 requires to come back for final approval.
  - Later decisions on closed buildings under real-property policy 8.70.040-P and directive 8.70.043-AD.
